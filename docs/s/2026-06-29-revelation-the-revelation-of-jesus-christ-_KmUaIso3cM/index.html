REVELATION: The Revelation of Jesus Christ

Date2026-06-29
Length00:36:13
Words6,968
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Revelation is a revelation of Jesus Christ showing that he has already overcome the world, written to encourage persecuted believers to remain faithful and discover how to live in light of his certain victory.

Summary

This opening sermon in a summer series on Revelation reframes the book as a revelation of Jesus Christ rather than a cryptic timeline of future events. The pastor urges the congregation to read it freshly—out loud, all at once—as the early church heard it, and to focus on how to obey and keep its message rather than decode prophecies about themselves.

Key points

  • Three goals: renewed perspective (promise not fear), renewed insight (careful reading), renewed mission (learning to obey what's written)
  • Read Revelation out loud in one sitting this week, as the early church heard it
  • Focus on Jesus at the center, not on finding yourself in the prophecy
  • Written to people undergoing persecution; uses apocalyptic imagery and prophetic language the original audience understood
  • Multiple orthodox schools of interpretation exist; the key is seeing Christ's ultimate victory and learning how to live faithfully
