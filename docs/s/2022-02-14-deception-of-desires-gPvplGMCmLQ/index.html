Deception of Desires

Date2022-02-14
Length00:44:43
Words7,462
SeriesJAMES
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Temptation deceives us into believing we can satisfy our desires apart from God's design, leading to destruction, but Jesus has secured our victory by perfectly resisting temptation and empowers us through the Holy Spirit to speak truth to lies in the moment between desire and disobedience.

Summary

This sermon explores how temptation works, tracing the pattern from desire to deception to disobedience and ultimately destruction. The preacher contrasts trials (which produce spiritual maturity) with temptations (which lead to death), showing how Jesus succeeded where Adam and Eve failed, securing victory for believers.

Key points

  • Temptation follows a pattern: desire leads to deception, deception to disobedience, disobedience to destruction
  • The source of temptation is our own desires, not God; between desire and sin is where we believe lies
  • Jesus was tempted like us but spoke truth to temptation, securing our final victory
  • We must identify the moment between desire and disobedience and speak truth to the lies
  • Victory comes through believing God is good and has a better plan for satisfying our desires
