Big idea

Biblical love, supremely modeled in Christ's incarnation and crucifixion, calls believers to sacrificial service toward neighbors and especially fellow believers, demonstrating love through action rather than mere affection.

Summary

This Advent sermon contrasts biblical love with cultural distortions, emphasizing that true love is sacrificial action rather than feeling or desire. Using Jesus' incarnation and crucifixion as the supreme model, the preacher calls believers to love their neighbors and, more radically, to love one another as Christ loved us—through self-giving service and sacrifice.

Key points

  • Biblical love is action and service, not emotion or personal desire
  • Jesus' incarnation demonstrates ultimate sacrificial love—leaving perfect communion to enter broken humanity
  • Two commands: love your neighbor as yourself, and the more radical command to love one another as Christ loved us
  • Spiritual worship is reasonable service—loving through sacrifice glorifies God
  • Examples of sacrificial love: Mother Teresa, St. Francis, Antonina Zabinska, Chernobyl workers
