Big idea

Christians are called to serve both intentionally in the specific areas God has prepared for them and interruptibly by creating margin to respond when the Spirit prompts, following Christ's example of planned mission and compassionate availability.

Summary

This sermon teaches that Christians should serve both intentionally—pursuing specific callings God has prepared for them—and interruptibly, remaining available to meet needs the Holy Spirit brings across their path. Using Jesus' deliberate journey through Samaria and his willingness to stop for blind men on the way to Jerusalem, the preacher argues that overbusy Christians miss divine appointments because they are overstimulated by needs and too distracted to listen.

Key points

  • Jesus intentionally went through Samaria to fulfill his calling to unite northern and southern tribes
  • Jesus stopped for blind men despite being at the height of his ministry, modeling interruptibility
  • Discern your calling at the intersection of person (gifts/abilities), place (where you live/work), and passion (what breaks your heart)
  • Cultivate interruptibility by ruthlessly eliminating hurry and creating margin in home, church, and work
  • We cannot do everything, but God has prepared specific good works in advance for each of us to do
