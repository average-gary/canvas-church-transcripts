Christoformity

Date2024-09-02
Length00:42:52
Words6,926
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

The ultimate purpose of the Christian life is Christoformity—being completely reshaped into the image of Jesus through the Spirit's work in community with His body, the church.

Summary

This sermon unpacks the theological term 'Christoformity,' which means being conformed to the image of Christ. The speaker argues that the goal of salvation is not merely getting to heaven but being transformed into the likeness of Jesus in every area of life, both now and for eternity.

Key points

  • Christoformity is being predestined and shaped to reflect Jesus' life, ministry, death, and resurrection
  • Knowing Jesus through Scripture, especially the Gospels, is essential to being formed like Him
  • Transformation happens in community—no one is conformed to Christ alone
  • The Christian life involves ongoing obedience, confession, and repentance, not just a one-time decision
  • Communion is the central practice that both remembers and re-members us into Christ's body
