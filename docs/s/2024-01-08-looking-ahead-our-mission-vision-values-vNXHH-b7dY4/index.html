Big idea

The church exists to create an authentic Christ-centered community where real people can encounter Jesus, so that God's kingdom becomes more visible through both bold proclamation and sacrificial obedience.

Summary

This sermon casts vision for Canvas Community Church's 2024 direction by unpacking their mission, vision, and core values. The pastor emphasizes creating an authentic Christ-centered community that welcomes real people with real struggles, prioritizes kingdom growth over church growth, and unites around the gospel while holding secondary theological opinions with an open hand.

Key points

  • Mission: invite real people into Christ-centered community
  • Vision: cultivate God's kingdom by proclaiming and obeying Jesus
  • Unite for the gospel, holding secondary theology loosely
  • Prioritize authentic relationships over event attendance
  • Pursue multiplication and church partnerships over expansion
