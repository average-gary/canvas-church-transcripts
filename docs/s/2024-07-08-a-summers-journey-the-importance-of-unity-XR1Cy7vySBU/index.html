Big idea

Unity among diverse believers is non-negotiable; we must accept and refrain from judging one another over disputable matters because Christ has accepted us all and will be our ultimate judge.

Summary

This sermon addresses the challenge of maintaining unity in a diverse church where believers disagree on consequential but disputable matters. Using Romans 14, the pastor argues that while Christians will have different convictions on many issues, unity under Christ's lordship is non-negotiable and must be pursued by accepting one another as God accepts us.

Key points

  • Romans 1-11 calls us to look to Jesus; Romans 12-16 applies that theology to how we treat one another
  • Distinguish between non-negotiable gospel truths and disputable matters where Christians can honorably disagree
  • The 'strong' are comfortable with freedom and gray areas; the 'weak' prefer clear boundaries—both can honor God
  • Accept one another as family because God in Christ accepts you
  • Don't judge one another over opinions because God will judge both groups
