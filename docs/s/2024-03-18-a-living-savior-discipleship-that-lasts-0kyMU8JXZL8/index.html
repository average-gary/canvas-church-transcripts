Big idea

Discipleship to Jesus that lasts is rooted in sacrificial worship and total surrender to God's agenda above our own.

Summary

This sermon examines Mary's anointing of Jesus in Mark 14, contrasting her sacrificial worship and total surrender with the various characters who opposed or missed God's agenda. The preacher challenges listeners to identify which character's heart posture they most resemble and calls them to follow Mary's example of complete surrender to Christ.

Key points

  • Chief priests and scribes plotted against Jesus because he threatened their power, wealth, and religious control
  • The disciples judged Mary's sacrificial act as wasteful, revealing self-righteous hearts despite following Jesus
  • Judas followed Jesus expecting worldly success and betrayed him when those expectations failed
  • Mary alone understood Jesus would die and demonstrated ideal discipleship through costly, surrendered worship
  • The burial oil's aroma would become a trigger reminding disciples of resurrection hope in future encounters with death
