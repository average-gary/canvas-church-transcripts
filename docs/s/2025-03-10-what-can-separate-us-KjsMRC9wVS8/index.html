What Can Separate Us?

Date2025-03-10
Length00:29:56
Words4,289
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Absolutely nothing in all creation—not death, life, spiritual forces, present circumstances, future fears, or any power—can separate believers from the love of God in Christ Jesus, and this unshakeable truth should free us from anxiety and empower us to love others radically.

Summary

This sermon explores Romans 8:38-39, unpacking Paul's declaration that nothing in all creation can separate us from God's love in Christ Jesus. The speaker addresses anxiety and fear by examining each phrase of this passage—death, life, angels, demons, present, future, powers, height, depth—to demonstrate the absolute security believers have in God's love, while also calling them to live transformed lives that reflect this confidence.

Key points

  • Neither death nor life can separate us from God's love; remembering our mortality right-sizes our anxieties
  • No spiritual forces, powers, or principalities can stand between us and God's loving embrace
  • God cares for both the universal (all creation held together in Christ) and the particular (individual needs like birds and flowers)
  • Believing in God's inseparable love should revolutionize how we live and love others, empowering us to see God's image in everyone
  • Though we struggle with unbelief and anxiety, we can trust Christ has overcome the world
