Big idea

The resurrected Jesus lovingly confronts our stubborn certainty and ideological blindness to reveal His true character and redirect us to the unique calling He has for our lives.

Summary

This sermon examines Saul's conversion on the road to Damascus as an example of how Jesus confronts spiritual blindness caused by stubborn certainty. The pastor explores how Saul was so convinced of his religious ideology that he persecuted Christians, yet Jesus lovingly intervened to correct his theology and redirect his calling.

Key points

  • Saul was blinded by religious zeal, persecuting Christians while thinking he served God
  • Jesus confronted Saul with two key questions: 'Why are you persecuting me?' and called him to ask 'Who are you?' and 'What do you want me to do?'
  • Three forms of blindness afflict believers: religious ideology, cultural/political ideology, and selfish desires
  • God uniquely positions each believer with gifts and calling to proclaim the gospel
  • We must regularly ask God who He truly is and what He calls us to do, then listen and obey
