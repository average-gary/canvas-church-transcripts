GO & TELL: Why Serve?

Date2025-01-27
Length00:48:48
Words7,690

VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Because Jesus has made every believer a new creation and royal priest, we are all called to serve within the church using our unique gifts and experiences, trusting that God uses our failures plunged into his grace to minister to others.

Summary

This sermon explores what it means to be a 'living sacrifice' (Romans 12:1) and how every believer is called to serve within the church as prophets, priests, and ambassadors. The children's pastor challenges the congregation that because the gospel transforms us into new creations and royal priests, we all have a calling to use our gifts and even our failures to minister to others.

Key points

  • The gospel should go deeper than surface-level belief, continually transforming how we see the world through Jesus' eyes
  • A 'living sacrifice' is paradoxical—an ongoing choice to die to self and live for God, not a one-time act
  • All believers are now 'royal priests' and 'living stones' in God's temple, not just religious professionals
  • Every Christian is called to function like Old Testament prophets, priests, and ambassadors—sharing the gospel, loving others, and holding each other accountable
  • Our failures plunged into God's grace make us more qualified to serve, not less
