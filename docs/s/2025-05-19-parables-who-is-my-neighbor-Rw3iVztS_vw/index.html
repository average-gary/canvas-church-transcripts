PARABLES: Who is My Neighbor?

Date2025-05-19
Length00:46:59
Words7,687
SeriesParables
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

True eternal life is found in knowing, loving, and obeying the God revealed in Jesus by extending sacrificial love to all image-bearers without redefining who counts as our neighbor.

Summary

This sermon examines the parable of the Good Samaritan to challenge the question 'who is my neighbor?' and reframe it as 'am I acting like a neighbor?' The preacher confronts how Christians use theology to justify limited compassion and dehumanize certain groups, while also revealing the deeper gospel layer: Jesus is the unexpected Samaritan who rescues us when we are half-dead in sin.

Key points

  • Stop asking who qualifies as your neighbor and start asking if you are being neighborly
  • Good theology is worthless without obedience and compassionate action
  • Christians often redefine 'neighbor' or 'love' to preserve comfort and justify inaction
  • The Samaritan foreshadows Jesus as the unexpected hero who sacrificially saves the dying
  • We are either the half-dead man needing rescue or the innkeeper entrusted to care for others
