Big idea

God accomplishes His kingdom purposes when His people unite around the gospel, lay down personal preferences, and faithfully serve others with their gifts.

Summary

This celebration service for Canvas Church's three-year anniversary focuses on how God has worked through the merger of two churches and the sacrificial service of volunteers. The message emphasizes that unity and fruitfulness come when believers lay down their preferences and priorities to put Christ and His kingdom first.

Key points

  • Church unity requires laying down preferences, privileges, and priorities for the sake of the gospel
  • God works through ordinary people who say yes to serving with their spiritual gifts
  • The church exists to create a Christ-centered community where weary people find rest in Jesus
  • Volunteers are essential to cultivating God's kingdom both inside and outside the church walls
  • Sacrificial love and dying to self allows God to do extraordinary things through His people
