Big idea

True peace that passes understanding comes from trusting Jesus with complete leadership of our lives, even through our darkest valleys and when surrounded by enemies.

Summary

This Advent sermon explores how Christians can experience biblical peace in the midst of difficult circumstances and opposition. The pastor argues that true peace comes from submitting to God's leadership rather than trying to control our own lives, using Psalm 23 to illustrate how God leads us as both shepherd through dark valleys and king who prepares a feast in the presence of our enemies.

Key points

  • Peace is creation in complete alignment with God's design
  • We take back control from God when circumstances get difficult, which robs us of peace
  • God as shepherd leads us through dark valleys to green pastures
  • God as king prepares victory over our enemies even when we feel surrounded
  • Jesus' birth established a kingdom that brings lasting peace through his death and resurrection
