Big idea

Jesus offers a peace that transcends all circumstances and understanding, accessed through dwelling with the Holy Spirit via prayer, gratitude, and focusing on God's goodness rather than life's troubles.

Summary

This sermon explores peace as the third fruit of the Spirit in a series on Galatians 5:22. The preacher contrasts worldly peace—which depends on controlling favorable circumstances—with the peace Jesus offers, which transcends circumstances regardless of external conditions. Through John 14 and Philippians 4, he argues that Christ's peace is accessed not by managing life perfectly but through prayer, gratitude, and focusing on God's goodness.

Key points

  • The world is experiencing an unprecedented crisis of peace and mental health
  • Worldly peace requires favorable circumstances we cannot control
  • Jesus offers a peace that transcends circumstances, not just copes with them
  • This peace is accessed through prayer, thanksgiving, and meditating on what is good
  • The story of Horatio Spafford illustrates peace in the midst of tragedy
