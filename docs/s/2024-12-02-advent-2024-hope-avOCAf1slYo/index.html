Big idea

Hope is the confident expectation and intentional participation in God's promises, rooted in the resurrection of Jesus and calling us to say yes to whatever God asks.

Summary

This Advent sermon examines Mary's response to the angel Gabriel in Luke 1, arguing that she possessed biblical hope—not wishful thinking, but confident expectation in God's promises despite dire circumstances. The message challenges listeners to see hope as both confident expectation rooted in Christ's resurrection and intentional participation in God's work, just as Mary said yes to bearing the Messiah despite the personal cost.

Key points

  • Advent calls us to look back to Jesus' first coming with gratitude and forward to his return with anticipation
  • Mary had confident hope God would send the Messiah despite being a poor young girl under Roman oppression
  • Biblical hope differs from wishful thinking; it is confident expectation rooted in God's character and promises
  • Our hope is anchored in the resurrection—the empty tomb proves God keeps his promises
  • God invites us to participate in his promises, not just believe them, empowered by the Holy Spirit
