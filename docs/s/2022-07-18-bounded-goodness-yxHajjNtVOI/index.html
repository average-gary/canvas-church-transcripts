Bounded Goodness

Date2022-07-18
Length00:49:59
Words7,879
SeriesFruit of the Spirit
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's goodness encompasses all His attributes and actions in perfect balance, which we cannot achieve on our own but only through radical dependence on the Holy Spirit, requiring us to stop redefining goodness to fit our agendas and instead walk humbly with God through the nuance of every situation.

Summary

This sermon explores 'goodness' as a fruit of the Spirit, arguing it is not a trivial concept but the summarization of all God's attributes and actions bound together. The preacher contends that humans constantly redefine goodness to fit their own agendas, but true goodness—perfectly demonstrated in Jesus—requires radical dependence on the Holy Spirit and cannot be achieved through human effort alone.

Key points

  • God's goodness is demonstrated in creation (Genesis 1-2) but shattered when humanity redefines it in their own image
  • When Moses asks to see God's glory, God reveals His goodness as the essence of who He is
  • Jesus is the perfect manifestation of God's goodness in human form
  • We cannot be good on our own—we must radically rely on the Holy Spirit rather than picking God's attributes that fit our narrative
  • Goodness means acting justly and loving mercy while walking humbly with God through every nuanced situation
