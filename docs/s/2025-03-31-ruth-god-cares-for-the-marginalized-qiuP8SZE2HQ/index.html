RUTH: God Cares for the Marginalized

Big idea

God works through the extraordinary generosity of ordinary, obedient people to bring redemption to the vulnerable, inviting all believers to embody his steadfast love in their daily lives regardless of their circumstances.

Summary

This sermon walks through Ruth chapter 2, showing how God providentially leads Ruth to glean in Boaz's field, where she encounters extraordinary generosity that goes far beyond legal obligation. The sermon emphasizes that Boaz embodies chesed (God's steadfast, loyal love) by treating Ruth not merely as a gleaner but as family, and calls the congregation to similarly participate in God's redemptive work through sacrificial generosity in their everyday lives.

Key points

  • Ruth and Naomi arrive at harvest time and Ruth enters Boaz's field through divine providence
  • Ruth boldly asks for more than the gleaning law requires, appealing to the spirit rather than the letter of the law
  • Boaz responds with triple generosity: permission, protection, provision, and table fellowship
  • Chesed (steadfast love) is God's defining character trait that motivates Boaz's actions
  • We are called to be benefactors of God's loyal love, regardless of our circumstances or resources
