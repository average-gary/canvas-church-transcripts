REVELATION: The Seven Trumpets

Date2026-07-27
Length01:07:45
Words9,155
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's escalating judgments are merciful warnings calling us to repent before the time of grace closes, urging us to abandon our idols and compromises with the world while there is still time.

Summary

This sermon walks through Revelation 8-11, explaining the seven trumpets as God's escalating warnings to an unrepentant world. Despite increasingly severe judgments, people refuse to turn from idolatry, yet God's patience has a limit and the time for repentance will close. The church is called to faithful witness rather than compromise with corrupt worldly systems.

Key points

  • The trumpets are warning shots showing God's restraint, destroying only one-third
  • Even after devastating plagues, people refuse to repent and cling to idols
  • The faithful sacrificial witness of the church, not punishment alone, softens hearts
  • The seventh trumpet closes the window for repentance and consolidates God's kingdom
  • Believers must examine where they've compromised with worldly values and repent now
