Big idea

Christian witness becomes tangible when we practice hospitality by making room for others in our ordinary lives, just as Jesus welcomed sinners to experience God's transforming grace.

Summary

This sermon teaches that Christian witness requires practicing radical hospitality—making room for others in the ordinary rhythms of our lives. Through the story of Jesus and Zacchaeus, the sermon shows how Jesus demonstrated God's kingdom by eating with sinners and welcoming the unwelcome, and calls believers to do the same by inviting people into their actual lives rather than trying to impress them.

Key points

  • Hospitality is making people feel wanted and welcomed in our ordinary lives, not impressive entertainment
  • Jesus sought and saved the lost by eating and drinking with sinners and outcasts
  • Biblical hospitality creates space where grace can be experienced tangibly before it is explained
  • Grace welcomes people before they are fixed, but also transforms them (as with Zacchaeus)
  • Practical challenge: invite one person into one existing life rhythm this week
