Big idea

True Sabbath rest is not merely stopping work but intentionally receiving rest as a gift from God that fuels our creativity and work throughout the week.

Summary

This sermon distinguishes between mere relaxation (turning ourselves off) and true rest (plugging into God's presence). The speaker uses the difference between 'remember the Sabbath' in Exodus and 'guard the Sabbath' in Deuteronomy to emphasize that we must be intentional about what we do during Sabbath to experience genuine rest rather than just distraction.

Key points

  • Relaxation is turning ourselves off; rest is plugging ourselves in
  • Deuteronomy calls us to 'guard' the Sabbath against distractions and busyness
  • Rest is a gift from God, not a reward we earn through hard work
  • We should work from rest, not work for relaxation
  • Examine what you're drawn to during Sabbath—does it fill you up or just distract you?
