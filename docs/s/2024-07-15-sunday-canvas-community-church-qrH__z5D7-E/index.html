Big idea

God saved us to change us, and he changed us to impact the world, which requires taking sin seriously within the church through loving confrontation aimed at restoration.

Summary

This sermon addresses how Christians should confront sin within the church community, based on 1 Corinthians 5 where Paul instructs the church to discipline a member living in sexual immorality. The message emphasizes that church discipline is meant for restoration, not condemnation, and must be done through loving relationships with the goal of bringing believers back to obedience to Christ.

Key points

  • Confronting sin in the church is for the good of the individual, protecting them from destructive consequences
  • Unaddressed sin spreads like yeast through dough, affecting the whole church body
  • Christians are called to judge sin inside the church, not outside, to be an effective witness
  • Church discipline should be a slow, prayer-filled process done in trusted relationships
  • The goal is always restoration, not punishment
