Big idea

Genuine kingdom service flows from an experiential understanding of how deeply Christ loves us, enabling us to serve others out of abundance rather than scarcity, guilt, or the desire for recognition.

Summary

This sermon explores Jesus washing his disciples' feet in John 13 as a model for sacrificial service done in hiddenness. The preacher argues that genuine kingdom service flows from deeply knowing God's love for us, not from guilt or a desire for recognition, and calls the congregation to start by serving friends privately before attempting more public acts.

Key points

  • Jesus washed feet while disciples debated greatness, modeling true kingdom service
  • We often serve with mixed motivations—guilt, recognition, or self-interest
  • We must experientially understand Christ's love before serving others properly
  • Hidden acts of service form us into people who can serve publicly
  • Start small: serve someone close to you in private this week
