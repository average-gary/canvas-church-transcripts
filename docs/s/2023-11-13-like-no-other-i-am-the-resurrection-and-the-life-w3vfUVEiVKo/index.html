Like No Other: I AM the Resurrection and the Life

Length00:39:33

Big idea

Jesus is eternally both the resurrection and the life, offering believers abundant life now in the midst of suffering and the promise of complete victory over death, sin, and sorrow in the age to come.

Summary

This sermon explores Jesus' statement 'I am the resurrection and the life' from John 11, addressing the tension between present suffering and future hope. The preacher emphasizes that while God chose life and resurrection, humanity chose death through sin, and now believers must respond by living into resurrection life today while awaiting its full culmination.

Key points

  • Jesus as both 'the resurrection' (future hope) and 'the life' (present abundant life) creates an 'already, not yet' tension
  • Physical, spiritual, and creational death all resulted from sin, but Christ offers victory over each
  • Believers should live into resurrection now through caring for bodies, fighting sin, praying for healing, and caring for creation
  • Eternal perspective enables Christians to face suffering with hope and live unfearfully
  • The new heaven and new earth will eliminate all death, sorrow, crying, and pain forever
