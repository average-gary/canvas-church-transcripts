Big idea

Effective witness flows from partnering with the Holy Spirit who is already at work in people's lives, not from our own persuasive words or efforts.

Summary

This sermon teaches that witnessing is not about bringing God to people through perfect words or arguments, but about bringing ourselves to God's work already in progress. The Holy Spirit does the convicting and convincing; our role is to rely on Him, stay attuned through prayer and Scripture, and courageously obey His promptings in conversations with those around us.

Key points

  • Witness is joining God's work already happening, not carrying the burden of results
  • The Holy Spirit convicts, convinces, and tells us what to say
  • Jesus did everything by the Spirit's power, not His own
  • Our role: rely on the Spirit, embrace simplicity, be courageous
  • Living in the Spirit through prayer and Scripture tunes us in to His voice
