Big idea

Christians should voluntarily lay down their earthly rights, preferences, and desire to win arguments to build meaningful relationships with people far from God, motivated by genuine love and running for an eternal reward.

Summary

This sermon explores Paul's approach to missional living in 1 Corinthians 9, calling Christians to voluntarily lay down their rights, preferences, and desire to win arguments in order to build genuine, loving relationships with people who need Jesus.

Key points

  • Paul's motivation: a genuine burden and love for lost people struggling under sin and death
  • Paul's strategy: cultural agility—becoming 'like' different groups to relate and build bridges
  • Paul's perspective: running for an imperishable crown, not temporary earthly prizes
  • Jesus modeled this first through the incarnation, laying down divine privileges
  • Application: prioritize relationships over winning cultural debates and political arguments
