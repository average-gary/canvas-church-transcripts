Big idea

Biblical hope is confident anticipation rooted in seeds planted through righteous action today, as Boaz's single act of integrity toward Ruth ultimately produced the lineage leading to Christ.

Summary

This Advent sermon explores biblical hope through the Book of Ruth, contrasting faith (present-focused) with hope (future-focused). The pastor shows how Boaz's righteous treatment of the destitute Ruth and Naomi planted a seed that grew into King David's lineage and ultimately Christ, demonstrating that hope is confident anticipation rooted in faithful action today.

Key points

  • Faith is in the present moment, but hope is in the future - Christians often struggle to live in hope
  • Ruth and Naomi were utterly destitute with no future during the lawless time of Judges
  • Boaz chose righteousness when no one was watching, redeeming Ruth's family instead of exploiting her vulnerability
  • This single act of integrity produced King David three generations later, saving Israel from chaos
  • The hope planted in Ruth's story ultimately bore fruit in Christ, connecting to Advent's anticipation
