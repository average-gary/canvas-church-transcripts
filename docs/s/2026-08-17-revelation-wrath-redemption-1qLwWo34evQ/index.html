REVELATION: Wrath & Redemption

Date2026-08-17
Length00:48:15
Words7,467
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's wrath against evil is an expression of His love for creation and will ultimately end because His redemption through Christ's cross has already secured the victory.

Summary

This sermon examines Revelation 15-16 and the seven bowls of God's wrath, arguing that divine wrath is not an embarrassing concept but a necessary expression of God's goodness and opposition to evil. The preacher emphasizes that God patiently desires repentance rather than rejection, and that His wrath finds its completion either in the cup of judgment or in the cup of the new covenant through Christ's atoning death.

Key points

  • God's wrath is good because God is good—it's His settled opposition to evil that destroys creation
  • God desires repentance, not rejection, and is patient in giving warnings before final judgment
  • The faithful stand victoriously on the chaos waters, singing of God's justice
  • God's wrath will end because His redemption through Christ has already won
  • Two cups are offered: the cup of wrath or the cup of covenant in Christ's blood
