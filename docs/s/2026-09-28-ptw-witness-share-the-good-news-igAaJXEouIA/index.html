Big idea

The gospel is an invitation to be re-narrated into the fuller story we were created for as image bearers and royal priests, and we share this good news by listening to others' stories and showing them the bigger story of God with gentleness and respect.

Summary

This sermon concludes a series on Christian witness by teaching that the gospel is not merely spiritual facts to add to our lives, but an announcement of the fuller story we were created for. Using Buzz Lightyear as an illustration, the pastor explains that just as Buzz needed to see he was a toy made to bring Andy joy (not just a space ranger), people need to see they are image bearers created for relationship with God and to demonstrate His goodness to the world.

Key points

  • The gospel re-narrates us into God's bigger story, restoring humanity's original vocation as priests and temples of God's presence
  • Through sin humanity shrunk from all being priests with creation as God's tabernacle to select priests in one location, but Jesus restores the original design
  • Effective witness involves listening to people's stories, affirming what is true, exposing where their story falls short, and announcing the gospel with gentleness and respect
  • Common worldviews like individualism, consumerism, nationalism, and achievement represent partial truths that need the fuller gospel story
  • We must know God's unified story from Genesis to Revelation to creatively share how Jesus invites people into their true identity and purpose
