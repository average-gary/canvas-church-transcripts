Big idea

True and lasting peace comes only when we submit every area of our lives to the rule and reign of Jesus, the Prince of Peace, rather than letting political ideologies, cultural narratives, or other influences govern us.

Summary

This Advent sermon explores biblical peace (shalom) as fullness and life as God intended, arguing that true peace comes only through submitting to Jesus as Prince of Peace. The pastor challenges Christians to stop letting political ideologies, screens, and cultural narratives rule their lives and instead rest in Christ, pursue enemy love, and become peacemakers in divided communities.

Key points

  • Biblical shalom means fullness and completeness, not merely absence of conflict
  • The quality and duration of peace depends on who or what rules your life
  • Christians must turn from screens and political tribes to rest in God's unconditional love
  • The church grows through sacrificial enemy love, not winning cultural wars
  • Believers should pray for peace for all people, even enemies, in global conflicts
