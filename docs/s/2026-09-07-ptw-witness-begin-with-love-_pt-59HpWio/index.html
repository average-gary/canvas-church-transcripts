Big idea

Followers of Jesus are called to joyfully and creatively witness to God's kingdom when we develop genuine compassion for others and become convinced that the gospel is actually good news worth sharing.

Summary

This sermon launches a series on Christian witness by reframing evangelism as joyfully demonstrating and declaring God's kingdom where we live, work, and play. The pastor addresses common baggage around evangelism and argues that effective witness flows from Christ-like compassion, a robust understanding of the gospel as kingdom announcement (not just afterlife insurance), and prayerful creativity in engaging neighbors and coworkers.

Key points

  • Our motivation must be genuine compassion (splaknizomai), not guilt, sales quotas, or proving ourselves right
  • The gospel message is the good news of Jesus's kingdom breaking in now, not merely where we go when we die
  • Witness today requires creativity and relationship-building, not just connecting doctrinal dots
  • We demonstrate and declare the kingdom naturally in our existing spheres of influence
  • The solution to bad evangelism is not no evangelism, but better motive, message, and manner
