Big idea

Because God cares about our priorities, our personal financial stewardship should reflect God's priorities by ordering our resources in the right sequence: giving to God first, then saving for family, spending responsibly, giving offerings to help others, and finally enjoying luxuries.

Summary

This sermon uses Haggai 1 to challenge believers about financial priorities, showing how Israel neglected rebuilding God's temple while investing in their own homes. Pastor Drew teaches that our use of money reveals our true priorities and introduces five biblical financial principles (Give, Save, Spend, Offerings, Luxuries) that should order our stewardship, calling listeners to transform from a self-centered 'upside down' approach to a God-honoring 'right side up' framework.

Key points

  • Israel's misplaced priorities: building their own paneled houses while God's temple lay in ruins
  • God uses difficult circumstances (scarcity, loss) to get our attention and realign priorities
  • Five biblical financial priorities in order: Give, Save, Spend, Offerings, Luxuries
  • Most people live 'upside down' (luxuries first, giving last) but need Romans 12:2 transformation to live 'right side up'
  • Money is a barometer showing what we truly love and value most
