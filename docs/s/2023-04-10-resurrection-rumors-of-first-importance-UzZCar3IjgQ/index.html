Big idea

The bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ is the essential, non-negotiable truth upon which all Christian faith rests, and those who believe it are called to live transformed lives of full devotion to God's work.

Summary

This Easter sermon examines 1 Corinthians 15 to establish the resurrection of Christ as the non-negotiable foundation of Christian faith. Paul presents a pre-existing creed—that Christ died for our sins, was buried, rose again, and was seen by witnesses—as 'of first importance,' arguing that without the resurrection, faith is meaningless, sins remain unforgiven, and believers are most to be pitied.

Key points

  • The gospel is non-negotiable: Christ died for our sins, was buried, rose on the third day, and appeared to witnesses
  • This creed predates Paul's letters and forms the foundation of apostolic teaching
  • Without resurrection, preaching is useless, faith is futile, and we remain in our sins
  • The resurrection validates multiple dimensions of salvation: payment accepted, victory assured, new life granted, redemption completed
  • Because Christ is risen, believers should stop sinning and give themselves fully to the Lord's work
