Big idea

Persevering through trials to finish life well requires keeping our affections on God, learning from past mistakes, refusing to make excuses, and trusting God's presence over our fears.

Summary

This sermon from 2 Timothy 4 examines Paul's final letter to show how believers can persevere through trials by learning from those who finished well and those who didn't. The pastor draws four practical lessons from Paul's farewell words about staying faithful when life is difficult.

Key points

  • Keep a close eye on your affections—Demas deserted the faith by falling in love with worldly things
  • Don't let past mistakes determine future faithfulness—Mark was restored to ministry despite earlier failure
  • Don't make lame excuses—Paul continued writing and ministering even from a cold prison cell at the end of his life
  • Don't let fear paralyze you—when everyone abandoned Paul at trial, God showed up and delivered him
