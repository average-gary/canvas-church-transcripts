Big idea

A healthy and effective church is built on individuals who are committed to Jesus-centered doctrine, devoted to prayer, submitted to the Holy Spirit, and invested in authentic biblical community.

Summary

This sermon launches a new series at a church merger, using Acts 2:41-47 to cast vision for a healthy, unified church. Ryan identifies four core attitudes that should characterize both individual believers and the congregation: commitment to Christ-centered doctrine, devotion to prayer, submission to the Holy Spirit, and investment in authentic biblical community.

Key points

  • Committed to core doctrine that keeps Jesus at the center, prioritizing relationships over secondary theology
  • Devoted to prayer both individually and corporately as the fuel for church health
  • Submitted to the Holy Spirit in both its curious supernatural aspects and common unifying work
  • Invested in authentic biblical community that is both effortful and vulnerable, not shallow or surface-level
