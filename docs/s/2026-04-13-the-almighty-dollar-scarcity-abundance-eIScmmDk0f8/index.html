Length00:43:43

Big idea

The illusion of scarcity produces greed and fear, but the abundance of God's kingdom produces generosity and joy.

Summary

This sermon exposes the illusion of scarcity that drives both greed and anxiety around money, contrasting it with the abundance available in God's kingdom. Jesus confronts a wealthy man's greed and his disciples' anxiety to reveal that life is not found in accumulating possessions but in trusting a generous Father who provides, inviting believers to live with open-handed generosity that makes the gospel good news to the poor.

Key points

  • Conversations about money reveal what we love and trust in our hearts
  • The scarcity mindset of 'life under the sun' produces greed in the rich and anxiety in the poor
  • God is a God of abundance who delights to give the kingdom to his children
  • Moving your money can move your heart toward kingdom generosity
  • The gospel is the true Firstborn distributing unlimited inheritance to all who receive it
