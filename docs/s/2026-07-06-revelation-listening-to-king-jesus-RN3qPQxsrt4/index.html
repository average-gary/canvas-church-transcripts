Big idea

The church conquers by listening to and obeying the voice of the risen King Jesus over every competing voice of deception, distraction, and discouragement.

Summary

This sermon examines Jesus's seven messages to the churches in Revelation 2-3, revealing that the risen Christ walks among his churches, knows their circumstances intimately, and calls them to give him their primary allegiance by listening to his voice above all competing voices.

Key points

  • Jesus is present and active among his churches, knowing their works, suffering, and failures
  • The church (ekklesia) is a political term indicating Christians are citizens of God's kingdom requiring ultimate allegiance
  • Three competing voices threaten the church: deception (false teaching and cultural compromise), distraction (loveless doctrine, ministry busyness, worldly success), and discouragement (suffering leading to despair)
  • Jesus responds differently to each threat: calling the deceived to repent, the distracted to wake up, and the discouraged to hold on
  • The repeated phrase 'he who has ears to hear, listen' emphasizes the difference between merely hearing Jesus's voice and actually giving it attention and obedience
