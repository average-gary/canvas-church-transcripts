GO & TELL: Who is Canvas Community Church?

Date2025-01-06
Length01:00:11
Words10,324
SeriesGO & TELL
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Canvas Community Church must intentionally make room—in schedules, homes, and the building—for God to work and for people to experience authentic, Christ-centered community.

Summary

Pastor Ryan unpacks Canvas Community Church's mission, vision, and values for 2025, using an NFL analogy of system, culture, and point of emphasis. The church's mission is to invite real people into authentic, Christ-centered community through four core practices: prioritizing real relationships, uniting for the gospel, relying on God's Word, and cultivating spiritual gifts to serve. He proposes 'make room' as the 2025 emphasis—creating space for God to direct lives and for new people to join, possibly through launching a second service on Easter.

Key points

  • Churches need contextual strategy, culture, and focus to fulfill the Great Commission
  • Mission: invite real people into invitational, authentic, Christ-centered community
  • Four core practices: real relationships, gospel unity, reliance on Scripture, serving with gifts
  • 2025 emphasis: make room for God and for one another
  • Growth is not the goal; obedience to the Great Commission is
