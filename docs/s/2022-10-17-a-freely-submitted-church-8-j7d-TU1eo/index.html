Big idea

Believers must learn to de-escalate conflict through wise use of words and spiritual maturity, keeping the unity of the body of Christ above personal preferences or offenses.

Summary

This sermon addresses conflict resolution within the church, emphasizing the importance of de-escalating tensions, using words wisely, and maintaining unity among believers. Using examples from Gideon, Paul, and the early church, the message calls for spiritual maturity that prioritizes peace over personal vindication.

Key points

  • Gideon's wisdom in de-escalating conflict with the Ephraimites
  • The power of words to bring life or death, peace or division
  • Spiritual maturity means mellowing and softening our positions
  • Distinguishing between the stated issue and underlying emotions in conflict
  • Paul and Barnabas's conflict over John Mark and eventual reconciliation
