SOLITUDE: Encounter with Our God

Big idea

Solitude is where we encounter God and actively surrender to His will and ways, which accomplish His kingdom purposes through the cross and sacrificial love rather than through worldly strategies of power, popularity, or political influence.

Summary

This sermon concludes a four-week series on solitude by teaching that withdrawing to pray is not just about self-awareness but about encountering God and surrendering to His kingdom strategy. Using Mark 1, the pastor shows how Jesus withdrew to solitary prayer even when crowds and disciples wanted Him to capitalize on momentum, revealing that God builds His kingdom through the cross and sacrificial love rather than through worldly methods of power, popularity, or political influence.

Key points

  • Jesus withdrew to solitude at the height of His ministry success to realign with the Father's will
  • The disciples wanted Jesus to follow worldly strategies for movement-building through crowds and popularity
  • God's kingdom strategy is the cross—sacrificial love and surrender—not power or control
  • Historical Jewish groups (Pharisees, Sadducees, Essenes, Zealots) understood the goal but missed God's strategy
  • Solitude is where we lock eyes with God and learn to accomplish His purposes His way, not ours
