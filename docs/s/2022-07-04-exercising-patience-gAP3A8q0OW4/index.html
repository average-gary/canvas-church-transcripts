Exercising Patience

Length00:38:33
Words6,414
SeriesFruit of the Spirit
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Christian patience—extending grace to difficult people and enduring difficult circumstances—is produced by the Holy Spirit as we grasp how patient God has been with us and trust His promised restoration.

Summary

This sermon examines Christian patience as two-fold: extending grace to difficult people and enduring difficult circumstances. In a culture of instant gratification and cancel culture, the preacher argues that true patience flows from understanding how patient God has been with us through our rebellion and sin. Jesus perfectly modeled this patience through His crucifixion, forgiving even those who killed Him.

Key points

  • Patience means not grumbling but extending grace to difficult people
  • Patience means enduring suffering like Job and the prophets
  • We underestimate God's patience toward us and withhold it from others
  • God's character is slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love
  • Jesus perfectly demonstrated patience at the cross, forgiving His accusers
