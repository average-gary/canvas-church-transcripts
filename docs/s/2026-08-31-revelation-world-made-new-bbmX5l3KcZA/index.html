Date2026-08-31
Length00:43:04
Words7,855

Big idea

God's eternal plan culminates in the New Jerusalem where heaven and earth unite, all creation becomes the Holy of Holies, and God dwells with redeemed humanity forever without separation, death, or curse.

Summary

The sermon examines Revelation 21-22, showing how John's vision of the New Jerusalem fulfills Old Testament promises from Ezekiel and others. The entire city is portrayed as an expanded Holy of Holies where God dwells with humanity forever, with no more death, tears, or separation.

Key points

  • John's Revelation parallels Ezekiel's temple vision during Israel's darkest hour
  • The New Jerusalem descends as a massive cube—the Holy of Holies expanded to encompass all creation
  • The river of living water flows from God's throne, fulfilling ancient prophecies and Jesus' promises
  • Believers prepare by drinking the water of life and washing their robes in faithfulness
  • The Lord's Prayer invokes this end-times vision of God's kingdom fully come
