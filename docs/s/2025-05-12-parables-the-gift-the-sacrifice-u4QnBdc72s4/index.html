PARABLES: The Gift & The Sacrifice

Date2025-05-12
Length00:51:00
Words7,608
SeriesParables
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

The kingdom of heaven is a free gift with ultimate value that requires sacrificial acceptance—laying down all competing allegiances to embrace God's rule and reign.

Summary

This sermon explores two parables from Matthew 13 in which Jesus compares the kingdom of heaven to a treasure hidden in a field and a pearl of great price. Ryan argues that God's kingdom is of supreme value—worth surrendering every competing allegiance—yet often misunderstood because it comes through suffering and sacrifice rather than immediate comfort or power.

Key points

  • Both characters in the parables found something of ultimate value and gave up everything to obtain it
  • The kingdom arrives in unexpected, upside-down ways that first-century Jews did not anticipate
  • Many people try to hold both the kingdom and other ultimate values simultaneously, but true discipleship requires singular devotion
  • The kingdom is a free gift, yet accepting it requires repentance (setting aside old values) and faith (embracing new allegiance)
  • Jesus himself modeled sacrificial love by laying down everything to purchase us into God's family
