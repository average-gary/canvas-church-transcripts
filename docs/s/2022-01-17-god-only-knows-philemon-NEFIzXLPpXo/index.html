Big idea

Christians should lean into difficult conversations with other believers, extending grace and working for unity in diversity, because such unity is the apologetic that demonstrates the truth of the gospel to the world.

Summary

Pastor Ryan teaches from Paul's letter to Philemon about navigating tense conversations between believers. Using the story of Paul mediating between Philemon and his runaway slave Onesimus (who became a Christian), the sermon outlines principles for pursuing unity amid difficult, emotionally charged disagreements rather than avoiding conflict or retreating into echo chambers.

Key points

  • Invite outside perspective into tense conversations rather than forming echo chambers
  • Build difficult conversations on a foundation of prayer and honor
  • Work hard to understand both perspectives in a disagreement
  • Let the gospel and the grace we've received be the unifying force
  • Prioritize continued relationship even after disagreement
