Abiding Love

Big idea

The love God calls us to is not something we muster through effort, but fruit that grows naturally as we remain connected to Christ and receive his unconditional, self-sacrificial love for us.

Summary

This sermon explores love as the first and foundational fruit of the Spirit, distinguishing biblical agape love from ordinary affection. The preacher argues that God's love is self-giving and sacrificial, demonstrated ultimately in Christ's death, and that believers bear this fruit not by trying harder but by remaining in Christ and receiving his love.

Key points

  • Jesus summed up the Law and Prophets with love for God and neighbor
  • Agape love is not emotion but self-sacrificial action, redefined by Jesus through the cross
  • God is the source of love; we cannot produce it ourselves but must receive it from him
  • John wrote powerfully about love because he was convinced Jesus loved him personally
  • Bearing fruit happens by abiding in Christ, not striving in our own strength
