Big idea

Christian unity amid diversity, centered on the gospel of Jesus Christ, serves as a powerful witness to an isolated and divided world.

Summary

This sermon on Psalm 133 explores Christian unity as a witness to a divided world. The preacher argues that true unity is not uniformity or avoidance of disagreement, but rather diverse believers united around the gospel of Jesus, becoming like sweet-smelling oil and refreshing water to those around them.

Key points

  • Unity among God's people should be rare and noticeable to the world, like pleasant perfume
  • True unity brings together diverse people around the gospel, not just those who agree on everything
  • Unity requires the miraculous work of the Holy Spirit and is a foretaste of heaven
  • Distinguish between closed-handed essentials (Christ's death and resurrection) and open-handed issues where Christians can disagree
  • Intentionally seek relationships with believers who have different perspectives
