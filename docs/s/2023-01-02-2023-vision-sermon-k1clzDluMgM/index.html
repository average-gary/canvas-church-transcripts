Big idea

God calls the church and individuals to faithful rebuilding by agreeing with His purposes, taking courageous action, making strategic plans, and maintaining humility as He brings transformation.

Summary

This vision sermon uses Nehemiah's rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls as a framework for both the newly merged church's organizational path forward and individual spiritual renewal. The pastor outlines four principles from Nehemiah—agreeing with God, taking first steps, making good plans, and handling success with humility—then applies them personally through a six-step repentance process.

Key points

  • Agree with God by confessing sin and aligning with His purposes
  • Take courageous first steps of obedience beyond mere agreement
  • Make strategic, flexible plans while expecting both momentum and opposition
  • Handle success with humility to avoid losing sight of God as the source
  • Personal transformation through recognition, repentance, renouncing, receiving, realigning, and rejoicing
