Big idea

Biblical discipleship is being with Jesus in relationship, becoming like Jesus through practicing his way of life, and doing what Jesus did by making more disciples.

Summary

This sermon introduces a three-year series on discipleship, arguing that American Christianity has made discipleship primarily intellectual rather than relational. Drawing on first-century Jewish apprenticeship models, the pastor presents discipleship as being with Jesus, becoming like Jesus through practicing his teachings, and doing what Jesus did by making disciples.

Key points

  • Discipleship is apprenticeship, not just information
  • Jesus invites the unworthy into relationship with the Father
  • Be with Jesus, become like Jesus, do what Jesus did
  • God's love is unconditional and desires our presence
  • Transformation comes through practicing Jesus' lifestyle and teachings
