Big idea

The Bible is a divinely inspired, unified narrative pointing to Jesus that must be understood within its human and cultural context to function as God intended—not as a simple rule book but as transformative wisdom that shapes every area of life.

Summary

This sermon introduces a series on using the Bible by establishing three foundational questions: what the Bible is, why it exists, and how to use it properly. Using the analogy of a T-post driver, the pastor explains that the Bible is a 66-book collection preserving one unified story pointing to Jesus, written through human authors but divinely inspired. Proper use requires understanding it as wisdom literature within its cultural context rather than as a simplistic rule book, allowing Scripture to transform believers from the inside out.

Key points

  • Like any tool, the Bible requires knowing what it is, why it exists, and how to use it
  • The entire Bible tells one unified story that points to Jesus as the fulfillment of Old Testament prophecy
  • Scripture is both divinely inspired and humanly written in specific cultural and historical contexts
  • The Bible provides wisdom for living rather than functioning as a black-and-white rule book
  • Deep engagement with Scripture transforms us through relationship with the Holy Spirit
