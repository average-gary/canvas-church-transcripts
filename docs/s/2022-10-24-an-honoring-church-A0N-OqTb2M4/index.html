An Honoring Church

Big idea

A church cultivates genuine affection when its members choose to honor one another by assigning the same high value to each person that God demonstrated by sacrificing Christ for them.

Summary

This sermon addresses how a church can move from merely obeying the command to love one another to developing genuine affection and familial bonds. The catalyst for this transformation is the practice of honor—choosing to assign high value to one another in the same way God valued us by sending Christ.

Key points

  • Paul's mission for the church: love each other, hate sin, hold to what is good
  • Paul's vision: move from commanded love (agape) to genuine affection (phileo)
  • The key behavior: honor one another by assigning high value to each person
  • God demonstrated our value by paying the ultimate price—Christ's blood
  • Compete to outdo one another in showing honor, not in seeking honor for yourself
