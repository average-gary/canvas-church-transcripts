Big idea

We must trust Jesus and his promises completely, even when his ways look different from what we expect, rather than settling for small gratifications that keep us from God's greater plan.

Summary

This sermon examines John 4:43-54, where a government official asks Jesus to heal his dying son. Jesus tells the man to go home, promising his son will live, and the official believes without seeing proof. The sermon challenges believers to trust Jesus fully rather than settling for lesser satisfactions out of fear or doubt.

Key points

  • The official trusted Jesus' word without needing physical proof or Jesus' presence
  • We often settle for 'mud puddles' instead of trusting God's greater plan
  • Jesus' healings reveal God's design for new creation, not just temporary relief
  • True belief transforms lives, not just intellectual agreement
  • The official's faith led his entire household to believe in Jesus
