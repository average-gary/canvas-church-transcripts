Big idea

Jesus is God's promised salvation freely available to all people, but many will reject him because they refuse to surrender control of their lives to follow him as Lord.

Summary

This Advent sermon focuses on Simeon, the faithful man who waited his entire life to see the Messiah and finally held baby Jesus at the temple. The message explores Simeon's prophecy that Jesus is salvation prepared for all people, yet will be rejected by many who refuse to surrender control of their lives.

Key points

  • After 400 years of God's silence, Simeon was promised he would see the Messiah before dying
  • God orchestrated multiple signs around Jerusalem confirming the Messiah's coming
  • Simeon prophesied that Jesus is salvation available to all people
  • Many will reject Jesus because of religion, intellect, possessions, or self-sufficiency
  • Following Jesus requires laying down our lives and giving up control
