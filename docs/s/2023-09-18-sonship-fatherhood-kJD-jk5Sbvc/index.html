Sonship & Fatherhood

Date2023-09-18
Length00:45:07
Words7,254
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

We can only walk out spiritual fatherhood after first receiving our identity as adopted sons who inherit everything in God's family not by earning it, but through Christ alone.

Summary

This sermon explores the reality of being adopted sons and daughters of God and the responsibility of spiritual fatherhood. The speaker shares personal struggles with earthly fathers and insecurity, explaining how we must put off the old self, receive God's unconditional love through Christ, and understand that through adoption we receive full inheritance in God's family without earning it.

Key points

  • The internal struggle between old self and new self requires us to deal with our brokenness as sons
  • Our souls are constantly being conditioned either for righteousness or evil through small daily erosions
  • God's love is paradoxically both unconditional and conditional—accessible only through Jesus
  • Discipleship must start at home, with parents as primary spiritual formers
  • The church desperately needs spiritual fathers and mothers to disciple beyond the home
  • Adoption into God's family gives us a new name, full inheritance, and a seat at the table we didn't earn
