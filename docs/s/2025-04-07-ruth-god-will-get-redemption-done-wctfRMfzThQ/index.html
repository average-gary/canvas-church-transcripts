Big idea

God will accomplish His redemption plan whether we participate or not, but there is far less collateral damage when we boldly partner with Him through faith and obedience.

Summary

This sermon examines Ruth chapter 3, where Naomi devises a bold plan for Ruth to approach Boaz at the threshing floor seeking redemption for their family. The message explores how God partners with people who take risky steps of faith to accomplish His redemption, while highlighting Boaz's godly character in private and addressing difficult topics of vulnerability, sexual ethics, and integrity.

Key points

  • Naomi's bold plan for Ruth to approach Boaz and request him to serve as guardian-redeemer
  • Ruth's courageous faith in going beyond the plan to seek full family redemption
  • Boaz demonstrates godly character in the secret place, honoring Ruth's vulnerability
  • God uses men and women working together in mutuality for His redemption plan
  • The question 'Who are you?' challenges believers to align private actions with public faith
