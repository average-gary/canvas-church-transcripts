Big idea

John recorded Jesus' signs and teachings to persuade readers to trust that Jesus is the promised Messiah and divine Son of God, so that through believing they might receive both eternal life and the abundant life God intended.

Summary

This sermon launches a 15-week series on John's Gospel by examining why John wrote it: to persuade readers that Jesus is the Christ and Son of God so they might have life through believing in him. The preacher unpacks John 20:30-31, arguing that biblical belief means trust and allegiance, not mere intellectual agreement, and that Jesus fulfills the Old Testament roles of prophet, priest, and king while offering both eternal life in the future and abundant life now.

Key points

  • John wrote his gospel so readers would believe Jesus is the Christ and Son of God
  • Biblical belief means trust and following, not just intellectual agreement
  • Jesus as 'the Christ' fulfills the Old Testament anointed roles of prophet, priest, and king
  • Jesus as 'Son of God' is the second Adam—fully God and fully man—who succeeded where the first Adam failed
  • Believing in Jesus brings both eternal life in the future and abundant life in the present
