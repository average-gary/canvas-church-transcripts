Big idea

Jesus, the most joyful person to walk the earth, calls his followers to choose joy as an act of defiance by trusting God's sovereignty even in broken circumstances.

Summary

This Christmas sermon teaches that Jesus was the most joyful person to ever live and calls believers to cultivate joy as an act of defiance against a broken world. Joy is not pretending problems don't exist, but choosing to view circumstances through the lens of God's sovereignty and eternal perspective.

Key points

  • Jesus was profoundly joyful—healing brought delight, children loved him, he feasted often
  • Joy is an act of defiance in a world that profits from fear
  • Believers must live in tension: the world is broken but God has triumphed
  • Practical steps: set your mind on joy, surrender control, pray daily, focus on good
  • Like GPS recalculating, respond to life's detours with trust instead of panic
