Big idea

Church membership is the practical way committed followers of Jesus identify themselves as living stones being built together on Christ's foundation into a temple that reveals God to the watching world.

Summary

This sermon makes the case for why Canvas Church has formal membership, arguing that in a culture that glamorizes independence, membership provides a practical framework to identify who is committed to doing life together as the local church. Using the image of living stones being built into a temple on the foundation of Christ, the pastor challenges listeners to consider how their lives and community might be different if they committed to a local body of believers rather than remaining isolated.

Key points

  • Culture glamorizes individualism, creating tension with church commitment
  • Membership distinguishes committed members from spiritual consumers and the spiritually curious
  • Church as living stones built together on Christ the cornerstone into a temple
  • Membership makes leadership harder but creates diverse, beautiful community
  • Committing to imperfect people is better than isolated independence

Scripture

1 Peter 2:4-8
