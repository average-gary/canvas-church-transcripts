Big idea

Jesus had to ascend to the Father's right hand so the Holy Spirit could be sent to empower all believers as witnesses of the gospel everywhere until the end of time.

Summary

This sermon explores the theological significance of Christ's ascension, distinguishing it from resurrection and emphasizing that only God can cause someone to ascend to heaven. The speaker argues that the ascension is an overlooked but vital doctrine that enabled the Holy Spirit to come to all believers and made Jesus the ongoing mediator between heaven and earth.

Key points

  • Ascension differs from resurrection: only God can cause ascension, not human effort
  • Old Testament foreshadowing through Elijah's ascent and Moses' mountain encounters
  • Jesus' ascension was necessary for the Holy Spirit to come to all believers
  • Heaven and earth converge in Jesus, who remains active as mediator at God's right hand
  • The call to witness extends geographically and temporally to the ends of the earth and end of time
