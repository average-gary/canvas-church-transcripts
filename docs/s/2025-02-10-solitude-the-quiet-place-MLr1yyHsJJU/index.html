Big idea

Solitude is intentionally making room for God free from all inputs or agendas, a practice Jesus modeled and invites us into so we can hear his voice and be transformed into Christ-like love.

Summary

This sermon introduces a series on silence and solitude as spiritual practices, arguing that Jesus modeled and invited his followers to regularly withdraw from busyness to lonely places for prayer. The practice positions us to hear from God and be transformed, though Western culture's emphasis on efficiency and constant distraction makes it difficult.

Key points

  • Jesus regularly withdrew to lonely places (eremos) to pray despite his busy ministry
  • Solitude is an invitation from Jesus to all followers, not extra credit for spiritual elites
  • Western culture's busyness and digital distraction prevent us from hearing God's whisper
  • Solitude means intentionally making room for God free from all inputs or agendas
  • Practicing solitude requires sacrificial decisions to eliminate hurry and reclaim margin
