Big idea

Fasting is a spiritual training tool that wakes us from dependence on earthly pleasures and trains our bodies to align with our spirits, enabling us to grow closer to God and overcome sin through His strength rather than our willpower.

Summary

This sermon presents fasting as a spiritual discipline that trains believers to deny earthly pleasures and align their bodies with the Holy Spirit. The speaker argues that Western culture's focus on instant gratification numbs people to God's voice, while fasting wakes us up spiritually and teaches us to depend on God's strength rather than worldly comforts. Through personal testimony, the pastor describes how fasting transformed her ability to resist temptation, remain content in discomfort, and experience deeper intimacy with Jesus.

Key points

  • Fasting weans us off the 'pleasure principle' of instant gratification
  • Our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit but also infected with sin
  • Fasting is a training tool that aligns body with spirit
  • Fasting reveals our weaknesses and reorders our desires toward God
  • Self-denial follows Jesus' example of sacrifice unto the cross
