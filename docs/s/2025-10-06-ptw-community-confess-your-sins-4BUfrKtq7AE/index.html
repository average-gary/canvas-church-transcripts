Big idea

We confess our sins to God for forgiveness and to one another in community for healing, which frees us from shame and reconnects us to both God and each other.

Summary

This sermon explores the biblical practice of confessing sins not only to God but to one another within Christian community. The preacher examines how shame—rooted in the fall of Genesis 3—causes us to hide from God and others, and how the discipline of confession in trusted community brings both forgiveness and healing, freeing us from shame's grip.

Key points

  • True community requires vulnerability: sharing flaws and creating safe spaces for confession
  • Modern obstacles to community include busyness, transience, digital distraction, flakiness, and declining emotional intelligence
  • Shame is the deeper problem—originating in the fall—that disconnects us from God and others
  • Jesus frees us from sin and shame, reconnecting us through confession
  • We confess to God for forgiveness and to one another for healing
