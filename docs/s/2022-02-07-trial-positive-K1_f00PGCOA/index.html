Big idea

The positive outcome of various trials is spiritual maturity, which happens when believers pre-decide to view difficulty with joy because they know God can use any trial to conform them to the image of Jesus.

Summary

This sermon from James 1:1-4 teaches that followers of Jesus should pre-decide to view trials with joy, not as a natural emotional response but as a deliberate choice, because any type of trial—whether self-inflicted, caused by others, or due to living in a fallen world—can be used by God to produce spiritual maturity and conform believers to the image of Christ.

Key points

  • Pre-decide to have joy in trials because a positive outcome is possible
  • Various types of trials (self-inflicted, caused by others, effects of fallen world) can all lead to spiritual maturity
  • Trials are a 'cheat code' for spiritual growth—they conform us to Christ's image faster than good times
  • Spiritual maturity means becoming more like Jesus and lacking nothing, enabling us to help others in their trials
  • Jesus is our example and great high priest who suffered in every way yet chose not to sin
