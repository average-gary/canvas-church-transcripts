Big idea

The church matures into the image of Christ when every believer actively participates with their unique gifts, walking together in unity at the pace of relationship rather than settling for passive consumerism or false uniformity.

Summary

This sermon on Ephesians 4:1-16 teaches that the church must walk in unity despite diversity, with every member actively participating in kingdom work rather than being passive consumers. The urgent kingdom initiative moves at the pace of relationship, requiring humility, gentleness, patience, and bearing with one another in love. Leaders are called to equip the saints for ministry rather than doing all the work themselves, and every believer has gifts necessary for the church to function properly as we mature together into the image of Christ.

Key points

  • Walk in unity with humility, gentleness, patience, and enduring love
  • God's urgent initiative moves at the pace of relationship, not corporate speed
  • Every believer has been given diverse spiritual gifts necessary for the church
  • Leaders must equip the saints for ministry, not just do the work themselves
  • Unity in diversity leads to maturity as we grow into Christ the head
