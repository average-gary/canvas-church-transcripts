ALL IN: Your Soul's Health

Length00:43:47
Words7,118
SeriesAll In
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Unforgiveness is a destructive force that smothers spiritual health, and believers must move beyond willful forgiveness to Spirit-empowered heart-level release in order to experience the growth God intends.

Summary

Using the metaphor of rotting leaves smothering a plant, this sermon identifies spiritual obstacles that hinder growth in believers, focusing primarily on unforgiveness. Drawing from Matthew 18 and the parable of the unmerciful servant, the pastor teaches that bitterness destroys the soul and that true forgiveness is a Spirit-empowered process moving from willful choice to heart-level release.

Key points

  • Pride, judgmentalism, causing temptation, and unforgiveness are obstacles that smother spiritual growth
  • Confrontation should pursue reconciliation, not accusation
  • Unforgiveness is like a root that slowly destroys a foundation
  • God connects receiving forgiveness with extending forgiveness to others
  • True forgiveness moves through stages from naming the offense to blessing the offender
