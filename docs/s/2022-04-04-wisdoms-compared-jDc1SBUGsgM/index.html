Big idea

Christians must examine whether they are building their lives on God's wisdom marked by humility and peace, or on counterfeit worldly wisdom marked by selfish ambition and chaos.

Summary

This sermon from James 3:13-18 contrasts two types of wisdom that govern our lives: godly wisdom characterized by humility that produces peace, and counterfeit worldly wisdom characterized by selfish ambition that produces chaos. The preacher challenges believers to honestly examine which wisdom they are building their lives on through three diagnostic questions about source, behavior, and results.

Key points

  • Two wisdoms exist: one from God (genuine) and one from the world, flesh, and devil (counterfeit)
  • The source question: Are we stewards of God's kingdom or controllers of our own?
  • The behavior question: Does our life show humility or selfish ambition?
  • The result question: Does our wisdom produce peace or chaos in our relationships?
  • Jesus' invitation to take his yoke is rooted in his gentleness and humility
