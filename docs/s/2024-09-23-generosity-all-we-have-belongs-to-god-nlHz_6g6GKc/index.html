Big idea

All we have belongs to God, and we are called to be faithful stewards and caretakers of His resources, giving generously from hearts surrendered to Him rather than from obligation or expectation of return.

Summary

This sermon teaches that biblical generosity begins with understanding that God owns everything and we are stewards, not owners. The speaker challenges the congregation to shift from ownership thinking to caretaking, emphasizing that giving should flow from a surrendered heart rather than religious duty or the expectation of return.

Key points

  • God is the owner; we are caretakers and stewards of His resources
  • Jesus flips the master-servant paradigm by serving those who faithfully steward what He entrusts
  • Giving should be regular, proportional, sacrificial, voluntary, joyful, and motivated by discipleship to Jesus
  • Reject prosperity gospel (give-to-get); God blesses us to give more, not just have more
  • Practice listening to the Spirit about how to use resources
