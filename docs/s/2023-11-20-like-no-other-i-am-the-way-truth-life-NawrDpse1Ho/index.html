Big idea

Jesus is the only way back to God's presence through his blood sacrifice, and by following his example and believing the truth about God revealed in him, we can experience abundant life now and eternal life forever.

Summary

This sermon explores Jesus' statement 'I am the way, the truth, and the life' from John 14, explaining that it is not a weapon of condemnation but an open invitation to God's presence. Jesus' blood sacrifice provides the only way back to intimacy with God, fulfilling what the Old Testament tabernacle system foreshadowed, while his life demonstrates how to live in God's presence daily.

Key points

  • Sin separated humanity from God's presence, but God has always desired to dwell with his people
  • The Old Testament temple system with blood sacrifices was a shadow pointing to Jesus' ultimate sacrifice
  • Jesus is both the way back to God's presence (through his blood) and the example of how to live in that presence
  • Jesus reveals the truth about God's character, correcting the lies that keep us from approaching him
  • Abundant life now and eternal life in the future are available through trusting in Jesus alone
