Big idea

True satisfaction and abundant life are found not in accumulating more possessions but in contentment—being satisfied with what God has given us and using our resources to invest in His kingdom and help those in need.

Summary

This sermon addresses the human tendency to accumulate wealth for personal pleasure and spending, warning that greed is dangerous, deceitful, and distracting. The solution Jesus offers is contentment—being satisfied with what God has given us—and practicing simple living so we can give more away and invest in His kingdom rather than chasing after more possessions.

Key points

  • Jesus warns that greed is dangerous, deceitful, and distracting from the kingdom of God
  • Contentment means being satisfied in whose you are, who you are, and what you have
  • Simple living involves limiting possessions and expenses to live on less and give more away
  • We adopt cultural spending norms and chase satisfaction through stuff, but nothing earthly satisfies our eternal longings
  • Many Christians are greedy not for themselves but for their children, teaching them that satisfaction comes from possessions
