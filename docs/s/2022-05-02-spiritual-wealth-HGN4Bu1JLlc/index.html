Spiritual Wealth

Date2022-05-02
Length00:50:42
Words8,133
SeriesJAMES

Big idea

True spiritual wealth is found not in earthly riches or the absence of suffering, but in making God himself the consuming desire of our hearts, trusting that he is good even when life falls apart.

Summary

This sermon explores James 5:1-11, examining the danger of letting wealth consume our hearts and the call to patient endurance in suffering. The preacher connects both themes through the tension between temporary earthly circumstances and eternal hope, arguing that true spiritual wealth comes from making God—not money or relief from hardship—the deepest longing of our hearts.

Key points

  • Earthly wealth is temporary and can corrupt every area of life when it becomes our consuming focus
  • Jesus calls us to store up treasures in heaven, not on earth where they decay
  • Patience in suffering means refusing to give up on God, even when we don't understand why we suffer
  • Job's example shows that clinging to God as Redeemer in darkness can lead to both present and future restoration
  • God is compassionate and merciful, and the antidote to both wealth obsession and despair is making God our highest desire
