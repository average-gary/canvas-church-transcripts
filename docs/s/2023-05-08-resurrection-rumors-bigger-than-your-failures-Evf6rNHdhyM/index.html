Big idea

The resurrected Jesus draws near to us in our failures to remind us of our calling and restore us completely, not to disqualify us but to equip us for greater ministry.

Summary

This sermon examines Jesus's restoration of Peter after his three denials, showing how the resurrected Christ meets us in our failures. Using John 21, the preacher demonstrates that Jesus brings Peter back to the scene of his betrayal—a charcoal fire—to offer complete forgiveness, remind him of his calling, and restore him to ministry.

Key points

  • Jesus reenacts Peter's original calling with the fishing miracle to remind him he hasn't been disqualified
  • Christ brings Peter back to a charcoal fire (scene of his denial) for restoration, not condemnation
  • Jesus asks Peter three times 'do you love me' to match the three denials and offer complete forgiveness
  • True restoration requires addressing the root cause of sin, not just the actions
  • Jesus confidently predicts Peter's future faithfulness and martyrdom
