Like No Other: Healing the Hopeless

Date2023-09-11
Length00:41:23
Words5,983
SeriesLike No Other
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Jesus seeks out and heals even the most hopeless among us—those who have lost their identity in suffering—demonstrating that God's grace reaches people regardless of their ability to demonstrate faith.

Summary

This sermon examines the third sign in John's Gospel—Jesus healing a man crippled for 38 years at the Pool of Bethesda. The pastor argues that while Jesus saves the ashamed and the desperate, he also seeks out the hopeless: those so degraded in soul and identity that they cannot even muster faith or answer his question 'Do you want to be healed?'

Key points

  • The man (called 'Fred') had been crippled 38 years, isolated, and had lost his identity in his disability
  • Jesus' healing preceded any recognizable faith—he sought the man out rather than waiting for him to come
  • We must not universalize our own experience with Jesus or require everyone to come to faith the same way
  • Identity must be rooted in being God's beloved, not in disability, roles, or accomplishments
  • Praise and lament are spiritual disciplines for those trapped in hopelessness
