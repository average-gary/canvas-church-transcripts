Big idea

Jesus reveals that God is an everlasting Father filled with compassion who dishonors himself to redeem his wayward children, inviting us to receive that love and extend it to others.

Summary

The sermon explores the messianic title 'Everlasting Father' from Isaiah 9, explaining how Jesus both demonstrates and teaches what God the Father is truly like through his life and parables. Using the parable of the prodigal son, the pastor shows that God is a compassionate Father who dishonors himself to redeem his wayward children, inviting us to receive that love and extend it to others.

Key points

  • Jesus demonstrates what the everlasting Father is like through his actions and compassion
  • Jesus teaches what the Father is like, especially through the parable of the prodigal son
  • God is a compassionate Father who reaches out to us even in our rebellion
  • We are invited to receive God's love and then extend it to others
  • Love is not just intellectual understanding but tangible, sacrificial action
