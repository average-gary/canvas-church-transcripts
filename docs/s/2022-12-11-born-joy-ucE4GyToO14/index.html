Big idea

True biblical joy flows from Christ's presence and the Holy Spirit's work in believers who serve others, remain connected to Jesus, and trust God's purposes even through trials.

Summary

This Advent sermon explores biblical joy through a 'buffet' of passages, showing that joy is not merely emotion but a fruit of Christ's presence, service, and the Holy Spirit's work. The message traces joy through Jesus's birth, life, death, and resurrection, culminating in communion celebrated as an act of joyful thanksgiving rather than somber introspection.

Key points

  • Serving others with the right attitude produces joy in the Holy Spirit (illustrated by the church's service event)
  • The presence of Jesus brings great joy - from His incarnation to resurrection to remaining in Him as the vine
  • The Holy Spirit fills believers with joy, especially when stepping out to love and serve beyond comfort zones
  • Joy, hardship, and generosity are often companions in the Christian life (Macedonian churches as example)
  • Joy comes from the blessed outcome - enduring trials with hope fixed on salvation and Christ's return
