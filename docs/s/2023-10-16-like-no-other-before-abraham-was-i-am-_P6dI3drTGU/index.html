Big idea

God has always been, is, and will always be inviting His creation out of slavery that leads to death and into freedom that leads to life, and Jesus is that same Yahweh in the flesh.

Summary

This sermon examines John 8:31-59, where believing Jews turn hostile toward Jesus within 27 verses. The central moment is Jesus's declaration 'Before Abraham was, I AM,' identifying himself as Yahweh, the God who revealed himself to Moses. The sermon emphasizes God's consistent character throughout history: inviting His creation out of slavery into freedom.

Key points

  • Believing Jews quickly move from faith to attempting to stone Jesus
  • Jesus confronts their stubbornness about needing spiritual freedom from sin
  • They are deceived by religious identity rather than true relationship with God
  • Jesus declares 'Before Abraham was, I AM,' claiming equality with Yahweh
  • God's eternal nature is to invite people from slavery into abundant life
