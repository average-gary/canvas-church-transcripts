Big idea

Baptism is the culmination of believers' obedience—both those who faithfully share the gospel and the new believer who publicly declares their commitment to follow Jesus.

Summary

This sermon explores baptism through the story of Philip and the Ethiopian eunuch in Acts 8, emphasizing that baptism is the culmination of believers' obedience—both those who share the gospel and the new believer who publicly declares faith. The pastor walks through how baptism starts with obedient witness, is built on clear gospel understanding, and ends with a new believer's step of obedience.

Key points

  • Baptism starts with an obedient believer willing to share the gospel
  • Baptism is built on a clear understanding of Jesus's message and substitutionary atonement
  • Baptism ends with a new believer's public declaration of faith
  • Baptism results from many small acts of obedience by believers planting gospel seeds
  • Baptism is the beginning of discipleship, not the end
