Big idea

Biblical servanthood is a transformative discipline that allows us to partner with Jesus, letting the world see Him while simultaneously changing us through humble service and the willingness to be served.

Summary

This sermon explores biblical servanthood as a spiritual discipline, using the wedding at Cana as the primary text. The speaker emphasizes that true servanthood is about partnering with Jesus in His mission, allowing us to witness His miracles firsthand rather than just enjoying the blessings from a distance, while also requiring the humility to be served by others.

Key points

  • The servants at Cana witnessed the miracle while others only enjoyed the blessing
  • Healthy orthodoxy (right belief) leads to healthy orthopraxis (right practice)
  • Biblical servanthood requires humility and willingness to be served, not just to serve
  • Avoid self-serving, over-serving, and under-serving; align gifts with God's will
  • Service should point others to Jesus while transforming ourselves
