Big idea

Seek the kingdom of God above all else and live righteously, and He will give you everything you need.

Summary

Pastor Matt teaches through Matthew 6, exploring Jesus's question 'What do you want?' as a framework for understanding the Sermon on the Mount. He examines three areas where people seek fulfillment—admiration, wealth, and security—and challenges listeners to seek God's kingdom above all else as they enter the new year.

Key points

  • The eye as lamp: what we focus on determines whether our whole body is filled with light or darkness
  • Three false pursuits: seeking admiration through piety, seeking wealth through accumulation, seeking security through worry
  • Acts of piety (giving, praying, fasting) should be done for God's eyes, not public admiration
  • Where your treasure is, there your heart will be also
  • Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and everything needed will be provided
