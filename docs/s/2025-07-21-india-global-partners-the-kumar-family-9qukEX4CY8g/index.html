Big idea

Through the Holy Spirit's power, Christians become witnesses for Christ by living righteously and showing love even to enemies, as David demonstrated when he spared Saul.

Summary

Using the story of David sparing Saul in the cave (1 Samuel 24), this sermon emphasizes that God desires to make us people after His own heart, not just successful in ministry. Through the Holy Spirit's power, believers are called to show Christ-like humility and love even toward enemies, becoming living witnesses that others can read.

Key points

  • God's plan for David was to make him a man after His own heart, not merely to make him king
  • David's refusal to kill Saul in the cave led to his enemy blessing him and prophesying his kingship
  • The Holy Spirit empowers us to do what we cannot do naturally—love enemies and live sacrificially
  • Christians are 'living Bibles' that others read; our lives must demonstrate Christ
  • Each believer is called to be a witness and bring at least one soul to Christ
