Big idea

Christians must unite around Jesus and the essential gospel truths while humbly laying down their rights and preferences on secondary matters for the sake of love, community, and the kingdom of God.

Summary

This sermon explores how believers can unite around the gospel while humbly navigating theological disagreements. Using 1 Corinthians 8-10 as the primary text, the preacher argues that Christians must prioritize love and the gospel over being right about secondary doctrines, recognizing that all believers have blind spots and are at different stages of spiritual development.

Key points

  • We all have theological blind spots and need humility to recognize them
  • Scripture should be weighed by its proximity to revealing Jesus, the supreme revelation
  • Knowledge without love puffs up; we must prioritize building others up over asserting our rights
  • Spiritual maturity involves recognizing we can be adults in some areas and infants in others
  • Unity requires laying down preferences for the sake of weaker consciences and the gospel mission
