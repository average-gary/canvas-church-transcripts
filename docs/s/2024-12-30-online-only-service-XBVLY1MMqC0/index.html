Big idea

God calls us to redeem our time wisely by prioritizing Him first, spending intentional time in His presence each day, and aligning our lives with His will.

Summary

This end-of-year sermon encourages reflection on how we have spent our time in the past year and challenges listeners to redeem their time wisely by prioritizing what matters most. Using Ephesians 5:15-17 as the foundation, the pastor emphasizes walking carefully, making the most of opportunities, and understanding God's will through intentional daily time with Him in prayer and Scripture.

Key points

  • Walk carefully and wisely, being diligent and accurate in your daily life
  • Redeem the time by making the most of every opportunity God gives
  • Find balance by prioritizing the 'big rocks' (God, family, health) before the 'sand' (distractions)
  • Start each day with God through morning prayer and Bible reading
  • Embrace renewal and recommitment rather than guilt over the past year
