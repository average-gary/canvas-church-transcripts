BOTCHED MIRACLE AND RESURRECTION

Date2022-04-18
Length00:38:56
Words6,580
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's people often see God's plans with blurred vision, but the cross and empty tomb bring his plans into focus—we must lay down our lives and follow Jesus to the cross to experience true resurrection life.

Summary

This Easter sermon uses Jesus' two-stage healing of a blind man in Mark 8 to show how the disciples were seeing God's plans with 'blurred vision'—wanting a powerful Messiah without the cross, resurrection without sacrifice. The pastor emphasizes that Jesus' invitation to 'take up your cross and follow me' means laying down our lives in submission, and that resurrection life comes only through the cross.

Key points

  • Jesus' intentional two-stage miracle was a lived parable showing the disciples saw partially but not fully
  • The disciples missed Jesus' first clear teaching about his death and resurrection because they wanted power without sacrifice
  • Jesus calls us to repent—'get behind me'—and follow him to the cross, not just add him to our lives
  • The invitation to die and take up our cross is only appealing because Jesus went first and showed us the empty tomb
  • The cross and empty tomb bring God's plans into focus: victory comes through sacrifice
