Big idea

Christianity is not about tweaking moral behaviors but about dying to the old humanity deceived by the enemy and being resurrected into a new humanity in Christ, where renewed minds lead to reordered desires and righteous living that brings life and flourishing.

Summary

This sermon explores Ephesians 4:17-5:21, arguing that Christian transformation is not about behavior modification but about dying to the 'old humanity' animated by deception and being raised to a 'new humanity' in Christ animated by truth. Paul's teaching, rooted in Genesis, calls believers to put off their former way of life and put on their new identity as beloved children of God, which results in righteousness through renewed minds rather than willpower.

Key points

  • Old humanity is rooted in deception, leading to disordered desires and destructive behaviors; new humanity is rooted in Christ's truth, leading to reordered desires and flourishing
  • Specific sins addressed include words (gossip, slander, obscene joking), possessions (stealing, hoarding, envy), and bodies (sexual immorality, lack of boundaries)
  • Repentance means turning from the old way of being human and walking in the new humanity through faith and surrender to Jesus
  • New identity must be lived out in community, with believers reminding one another through worship, singing, and speaking truth in love
  • God's wrath is allowing rebellious children to suffer the consequences of their decisions, but His final word is mercy through Christ
