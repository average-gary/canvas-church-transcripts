Easter 2025: WHO IS JESUS?

Date2025-04-21
Length00:34:25
Words5,861
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Jesus draws near to the suffering and skeptical with an invitation of hope rooted in the resurrection, revealing through Scripture that he came not for temporary earthly deliverance but to defeat sin, death, and the grave eternally.

Summary

This Easter sermon explores the post-resurrection encounter between Jesus and two disciples on the road to Emmaus (Luke 24), focusing on how Jesus responds to followers who are suffering and skeptical. The preacher argues that Jesus draws near to those walking away in doubt, lovingly corrects their misplaced hope for temporal deliverance, and reveals through Scripture that all the Old Testament points to his mission to defeat sin and death eternally.

Key points

  • Jesus draws near to suffering and skeptical disciples who are walking away from where he called them
  • The disciples had hoped for political deliverance from Rome but missed the greater spiritual victory over sin and death
  • Jesus lovingly rebukes them and preaches through all of Scripture showing how it points to him
  • Their eyes are opened when Jesus breaks bread, combining Word and sacrament
  • The resurrection demands a response: will we walk away in skepticism or look to the one whose body was broken for us
