Big idea

While evidence and apologetics can remove intellectual barriers to faith, true abundant life is found not in satisfying physical or emotional needs but in the unshakable peace that comes from trusting Jesus as the source of eternal life.

Summary

This sermon addresses doubt and faith through the story of Thomas encountering the risen Jesus. The pastor argues that doubt is natural and can be answered through apologetics—rational evidence for Christianity from science, archaeology, and history—but emphasizes that the ultimate goal is not just intellectual certainty but experiencing 'Zoe,' the abundant, unshakable life found only in relationship with Jesus.

Key points

  • Jesus responds to Thomas's doubt with peace and proof, not condemnation
  • Apologetics (defense of faith) helps remove barriers to trusting God
  • Science, archaeology, and historical records corroborate Biblical accounts
  • Physical and emotional needs ('bios' and 'psyche') can never be fully satisfied
  • True abundant life ('Zoe') comes only through Jesus and brings lasting peace
