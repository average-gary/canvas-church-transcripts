Big idea

Crusaders are believers who become so burdened by a spiritual problem that they must take action to fix it, and the church needs their passionate, singular focus to address injustice and brokenness in the world.

Summary

This sermon explores the 'Crusader' spiritual station—believers who become so burdened by a specific spiritual problem that they cannot rest until they take action to solve it. Using Nehemiah as the biblical model, the message defines Crusaders as action-oriented followers of Jesus with a singular focus, then features three ministry leaders inviting the congregation to join them in hospitality, children's ministry, and fighting human trafficking.

Key points

  • Crusaders are action-oriented believers with singular focus who cannot move on until a spiritual problem is addressed
  • Nehemiah exemplifies the Crusader—burdened by Jerusalem's ruins, he wept, prayed, and took bold action
  • Strengths include getting things done, giving voice to the voiceless, and calling others out of complacency
  • Weaknesses include burning out, being polarizing, and rooting identity in works rather than in Christ
  • Three ministries presented: hospitality/first impressions, children's ministry and Kids Club, and Blazing Heart Ranch anti-trafficking work
