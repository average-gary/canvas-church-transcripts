SEASONS OF FORMATION: Life is Good, God is Absent

Date2024-04-15
Length00:45:39
Words7,751
SeriesSEASON OF FORMATION
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's presence must always take priority over our productivity for Him, and seasons where He feels absent are often invitations to examine our distractions, deepen our surrender, address unrepentant sin, or grow our spiritual roots through intentional practices.

Summary

This sermon addresses the spiritual season where life circumstances are fine but God feels absent, exploring four reasons believers might experience this disconnection: distraction from busyness and ministry work, lack of full surrender to God, unrepentant sin (especially unforgiveness), or God's invitation to deeper spiritual maturity and intimacy.

Key points

  • Presence with God must always take priority over productivity for God
  • Busyness in ministry can distract us from sitting at Jesus' feet like Martha did
  • Full surrender means giving God access to every room of our lives, not walling off certain areas
  • Unrepentant sin, particularly unforgiveness, creates distance from God
  • Like trees in drought that grow roots deeper rather than shoots higher, seasons of God's absence invite us to develop deeper spiritual roots through practices like Sabbath and solitude
