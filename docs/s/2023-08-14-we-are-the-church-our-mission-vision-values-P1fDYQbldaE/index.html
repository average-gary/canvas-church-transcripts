Big idea

Christian exiles in secular culture are called to live for the prosperity of the culture without conforming to its patterns or fighting against its people, proclaiming Jesus clearly and obeying him boldly.

Summary

This sermon teaches how Christians should engage with an increasingly post-Christian culture—not by fighting, withdrawing, or conforming, but by seeking the culture's welfare while remaining distinctly Christian. Using Jeremiah 29 and the example of Jewish exiles in Babylon, the pastor presents a blueprint for faithful engagement and unveils the church's mission and vision statements.

Key points

  • Three wrong responses to secular culture: fighting back (culture war), withdrawing into holy huddles, or conforming and losing distinctiveness
  • Jeremiah 29 calls exiles to seek Babylon's welfare while maintaining their identity—loyal subversion
  • Build balanced community: deep relationships with believers and intentional friendships with non-believers
  • Remain faithful even in difficulty; cultivate spiritual burden for the culture through prayer and presence
  • Mission: invite real people into Christ-centered community; Vision: cultivate God's kingdom by proclaiming and obeying Jesus
