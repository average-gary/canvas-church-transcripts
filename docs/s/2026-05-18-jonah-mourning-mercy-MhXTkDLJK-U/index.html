JONAH: Mourning & Mercy

Big idea

The hardest people to convince of God's mercy are often those who have already received it—we gladly accept grace for ourselves but struggle when that same mercy reaches people we consider undeserving or our enemies.

Summary

This sermon on Jonah 3 explores how Jonah, a second-chance prophet, reluctantly preaches to Nineveh and watches the city repent dramatically. The central tension is not whether God will show mercy, but whether Jonah—and by extension, God's people—can rejoice when divine mercy reaches those we consider enemies or undeserving.

Key points

  • Jonah receives a second chance from God but resents when that same mercy reaches Nineveh
  • Nineveh repents completely, from the king down, turning from violence and calling on God
  • God relents from judgment, fulfilling Jonah's fear that mercy would reach the wrong people
  • 'Jonah syndrome': gladly receiving mercy for ourselves while resenting when it reaches others
  • Jesus embodies mercy that moves outward beyond all boundaries, calling us to do the same
