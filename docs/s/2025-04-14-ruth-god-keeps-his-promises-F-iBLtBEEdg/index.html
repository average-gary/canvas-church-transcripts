Big idea

God accomplishes redemption through people willing to sacrificially love others at personal cost, ultimately pointing to Jesus who laid down his life to become our kinsman redeemer and invites us to do the same for others.

Summary

This sermon concludes a series on Ruth by examining chapter 4, where Boaz negotiates to become the kinsman redeemer for Ruth and Naomi. The preacher emphasizes how Boaz's sacrificial love—going beyond legal requirements at personal cost—foreshadows Jesus Christ as our ultimate redeemer who laid down his divinity to redeem humanity.

Key points

  • Boaz negotiates redemption at the town gate; another kinsman redeemer opts out when the full cost is revealed
  • Boaz combines the role of kinsman redeemer and levirate husband, a costly act of sacrificial love not required by law
  • The unnamed redeemer chooses earthly security over participation in God's eternal redemption plan
  • Ruth bears Obed, redeeming Naomi's family line and continuing the Messianic lineage to King David
  • Following Jesus requires laying down our lives to receive his abundant life, just as Boaz risked his estate for redemption
