We Are the Church: Our Mission

Length00:59:53

VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

The church fulfills the Great Commission not by reaching individuals one at a time, but by strategically partnering to reach whole people groups who can then evangelize themselves, investing locally, regionally, and globally with both resources and relational involvement.

Summary

This sermon outlines the church's mission strategy for ministry beyond its walls, emphasizing that at least 10% of the budget supports outreach in three concentric circles: local ministry through a Kids Club, regional work with Blazing Hope Ranch addressing sex trafficking, and global missions to unreached people groups in India. The pastor argues that the Great Commission calls believers to reach distinct people groups (ethnos), not just individuals, enabling gospel multiplication rather than mere addition.

Key points

  • At least 10% of the church budget goes to ministry outside the congregation
  • Focus on fewer ministries with larger impact, prioritizing relational equity over just financial support
  • Three main ministry partnerships: local Kids Club, regional Blazing Hope Ranch, global India missions
  • The Great Commission targets ethnos (socio-linguistic people groups), not just individuals
  • Reaching people groups enables multiplication rather than addition in evangelism
