REVELATION: God's Redemptive Story

Date2026-08-03
Length00:31:58
Words5,358
SeriesREVELATION
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Christians live in the tension between Christ's accomplished victory and final return, participating in God's story through baptism as initial faith and communion as ongoing faithfulness while the defeated enemy causes suffering in his limited remaining time.

Summary

This sermon examines Revelation 12 as a 'trailer' for the Bible's unified redemptive story, showing how the woman, child, and dragon have appeared throughout Scripture from Genesis to the present. The pastor connects this cosmic narrative to the ordinances of baptism and communion, explaining how Christians participate in God's story through initial faith and ongoing faithfulness despite the enemy's continued attacks.

Key points

  • Revelation 12 functions as a summary of God's story from Genesis through Revelation with three main characters
  • Satan has been decisively defeated and thrown down but causes suffering because his time is short
  • Baptism embodies passing through chaos waters to salvation, like Noah's ark and the Red Sea crossing
  • Communion sustains believers in the wilderness, reminding them to love not their lives unto death
  • Christians participate through faith (baptism) and faithfulness (communion) in the already-but-not-yet kingdom
