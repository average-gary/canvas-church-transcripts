Big idea

Jesus physically rose from the dead, established essential gospel truths with his disciples, and commissions all believers to be filled with the Holy Spirit and sent into the world just as the Father sent him.

Summary

This sermon focuses on Jesus' post-resurrection appearance to his disciples in the locked room, emphasizing both the physical reality of the resurrection and the essential gospel truths he wanted them to grasp. The preacher uses water metaphors and an object lesson to illustrate different dimensions of relationship with the Holy Spirit, and concludes by connecting communion to the call to be chosen, blessed, broken, and given for God's purposes.

Key points

  • Jesus' physical resurrection is a non-negotiable essential doctrine
  • Core gospel essentials: Scripture fulfillment, the cross, resurrection, repentance and forgiveness, taking the gospel to all nations
  • Relationship with the Holy Spirit has multiple dimensions: conviction, filling/drinking, and immersion/baptism
  • We are chosen and blessed by God to be broken and given for his kingdom purposes
  • As the Father sent Jesus, so Jesus sends us
