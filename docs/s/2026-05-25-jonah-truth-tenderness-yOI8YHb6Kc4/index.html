JONAH: Truth & Tenderness

Length00:47:18

Big idea

God perfectly balances justice and mercy through the cross of Jesus, and while we resist His truth and authority like Jonah, God patiently pursues us with tender love, calling us to surrender and extend the same compassion to others.

Summary

This sermon concludes a series on Jonah by examining chapter 4, where Jonah's anger over God's mercy to Nineveh exposes his disagreement with God's definition of truth and rebellion against God's authority. The preacher shows how God patiently pursues Jonah with tender questions and a lived parable, ultimately pointing to the cross where God's justice and mercy meet perfectly in Jesus.

Key points

  • Jonah partially quotes Exodus 34, omitting 'faithfulness' to challenge God's truthfulness
  • Like Jonah, we selectively use Scripture to create a God who affirms our ideology
  • Jonah goes east and builds his own shelter, symbolizing flight from God's presence and authority
  • God engages Jonah's heart through the parable of the plant, worm, and wind
  • God demonstrates both justice and mercy through Christ's sacrifice on the cross
