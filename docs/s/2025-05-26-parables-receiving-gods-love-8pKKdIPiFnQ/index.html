PARABLES: Receiving God's Love

Date2025-05-26
Length00:51:04
Words7,406
SeriesParables
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's love is not earned by our worth but freely given, and we must receive it like children to understand that this love—not our merit—establishes our identity, value, and belonging.

Summary

This sermon explores the parable of the prodigal son (Luke 15) to reveal that God's love must be received before it can be understood. Both sons misunderstood the father's love—the younger son failed to appreciate what it provided, while the older son believed it was earned through obedience—but neither grasped that love is freely given, not based on worth.

Key points

  • The younger son's request for inheritance was culturally equivalent to wishing his father dead, yet the father responded with humiliating himself to restore the son
  • Both sons thought the father's love was connected to their worth, but love is not earned—it is given because the father chose to love them
  • God's love establishes our identity, position, and belonging, just as a parent gives a child their name, home, and security
  • We must receive God's love like small children who simply believe, not rationalize or try to understand it intellectually first
  • God's love is more valuable than financial security, achievement, acceptance, or any other pursuit
