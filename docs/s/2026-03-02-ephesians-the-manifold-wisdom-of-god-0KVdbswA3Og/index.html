Big idea

The gospel's ultimate purpose is to unite diverse peoples into one church that, by embodying reconciliation and reigning with Christ above the spiritual powers, demonstrates God's manifold wisdom and victory to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms.

Summary

The sermon argues that the gospel is far more than individual salvation—it is God's cosmic plan to defeat spiritual rulers and authorities by uniting people from every ethnicity, culture, and political identity into one multi-ethnic church. This unified church, seated with Christ above the powers, demonstrates God's manifold wisdom to the heavenly realms.

Key points

  • The 'mystery' revealed in Ephesians 3 is that God unites Jews and Gentiles (and all peoples) into one body through the cross
  • Rulers and authorities in heavenly realms are powerful spiritual beings that animate earthly systems—political empires, cultural identities, economic structures—to steal worship from God
  • The cross was God's manifold wisdom: the powers thought killing Jesus would secure their victory, but it led to their defeat
  • The church's vocation is to live as a unified multi-ethnic family with Christ as primary identity, demonstrating God's victory over the powers
  • American Christianity has often given allegiance to political parties and cultural identities rather than Christ, failing to reveal God's wisdom
