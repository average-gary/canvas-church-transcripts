Like No Other: Raising Lazarus

Length00:47:29

Big idea

Jesus is the resurrection and the life, and by raising Lazarus He revealed His glory, set in motion His own death and resurrection, and calls each person to believe that death is not the end.

Summary

This sermon examines the raising of Lazarus in John 11 as the pivotal center of John's Gospel, the catalyst that set in motion the events leading to Jesus's death and resurrection. The preacher explores why Jesus deliberately delayed, let Lazarus die, and then raised him—to reveal His glory and enable people to conceive of resurrection before they could believe in His own.

Key points

  • Jesus deliberately waited two days so Lazarus would truly be dead (four days in the tomb) before raising him
  • Jesus declared 'I am the resurrection and the life' to Martha, asking 'Do you believe this?'
  • Jesus wept, showing both His humanity (grief) and divinity (power over death)
  • The raising of Lazarus prompted the Sanhedrin to plot Jesus's death, fulfilling Caiaphas's prophecy
  • This miracle enabled people to conceive of resurrection so they could later believe in Jesus's own rising
