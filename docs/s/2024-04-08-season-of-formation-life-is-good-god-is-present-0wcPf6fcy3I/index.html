SEASON OF FORMATION: Life is Good, God is Present

Big idea

God intentionally moves us through different spiritual seasons—not just times of delight—to transform us into the image of Jesus, and we must learn to respond faithfully in each season rather than escape discomfort or normalize only the good times.

Summary

This sermon introduces a series on spiritual seasons God uses to form believers into Christ's image. Pastor Matt focuses on the 'season of wonder' when life is good and God seems present, explaining it as an initial 'puppy love' stage that shouldn't be mistaken for the entirety of Christian experience. He previews four seasons (wonder, silence, desert, darkness) and emphasizes that God uses all circumstances—including difficulty and God's seeming absence—to make us more like Jesus.

Key points

  • God uses changing seasons in our lives to form us into Christ's image
  • The season of wonder is sweet and exciting but immature, like puppy love
  • Four spiritual seasons: life good/God present, life good/God absent, life hard/God present, life hard/God absent
  • We shouldn't normalize the season of wonder or condemn ourselves when we're not in it
  • God's grace is available in every season
