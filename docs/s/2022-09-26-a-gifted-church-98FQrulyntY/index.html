A Gifted Church

Length00:29:29

Big idea

Believers are called out as disciples and gifted back to the world to make more disciples together in unity.

Summary

This sermon explores Ephesians 4:1-13, emphasizing that believers are not merely given gifts but are themselves God's gifts to the world. Kent McKay traces how God took the Levites as captives and gave them back to Israel so He could dwell among them, then parallels this with how Christ sanctifies believers and sends them back as gifts to make disciples in unity.

Key points

  • Unity is central: one body, one Spirit, one calling to discipleship
  • Believers are the gifts God gives back to the world, not just recipients of gifts
  • Walk worthy of your calling with humility, gentleness, and patience
  • Different giftings (apostles, prophets, pastors, teachers) must work together in unity
  • The mission: called out as disciples, gifted back to make more disciples together
