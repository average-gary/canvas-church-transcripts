Big idea

Believers should be completely committed to Christ without complaint, engaging the world for the gospel rather than shrinking their sphere of concern to avoid discomfort, following Paul's example of joyful sacrifice even unto death.

Summary

This sermon, concluding a series on Paul, examines Philippians 2:14-30 where Paul writes from prison possibly facing martyrdom. Using a dramatic opening about a man who shrinks his 'cosmos' to avoid discomfort, the preacher challenges believers to be 'all in' for Christ like Paul, Timothy, and Epaphroditus, especially in reaching the billions who have no access to the gospel.

Key points

  • Paul commands believers to do everything without grumbling or arguing despite living in a warped generation
  • Paul describes himself being 'poured out like a drink offering' as he faces potential martyrdom with joy
  • Three examples of total commitment: Paul the apostle, Timothy the pastor, and Epaphroditus the messenger
  • The question: Are we more like Alan (shrinking our world) or like Christ's faithful witnesses?
  • Billions live without access to the gospel, requiring believers to be 'all in' rather than asking 'should I go' but 'why should I stay'
