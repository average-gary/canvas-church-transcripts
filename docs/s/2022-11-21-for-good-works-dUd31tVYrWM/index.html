Big idea

God has created every believer in Christ Jesus with a unique set of good works prepared in advance, and together we form a collective masterpiece that reveals His character and advances His kingdom to the world.

Summary

This sermon concludes a series on Ephesians 2:10, emphasizing that every believer is created in Christ Jesus for unique good works that God has prepared in advance. Using a puzzle illustration, the pastor argues that each Christian has an irreplaceable part to play in revealing God's character and advancing His kingdom to a watching world.

Key points

  • Every believer is called into ministry, not just pastors or church leaders
  • Past failures and brokenness do not disqualify you; God uses our weakness for His purposes
  • Good works serve as specific revelation, demonstrating God's character and proclaiming the gospel through actions and words
  • Discovering your good works involves examining your personality, opportunities, spiritual gifts, listening prayer, and seeking godly counsel
  • We are individual puzzle pieces forming a collective masterpiece that displays the kingdom of God
