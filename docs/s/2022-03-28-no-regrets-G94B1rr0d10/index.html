Big idea

Because our words reveal what fills our hearts and have power to build up or destroy lives, we must allow Christ to transform our hearts so we view others through His eyes and speak without regret.

Summary

This sermon on James 3:1-12 explores why controlling our tongues is so difficult despite knowing words have immense power. The speaker argues that we struggle to tame our speech because we feel justified in our anger, but God calls us to leave justice to Him and view others through Christ's eyes rather than our own hurt.

Key points

  • Words have power to direct the course of lives, like a bit steers a horse or a rudder steers a ship
  • No human can control the tongue in their own strength—only through Christ's power living in us
  • We often fail to control our words because we feel justified when hurt or wronged
  • God alone provides justice in His timing; we must not repay evil for evil
  • What we speak flows from what fills our hearts—transformation begins there
