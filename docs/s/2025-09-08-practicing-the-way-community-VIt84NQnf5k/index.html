Big idea

Jesus welcomed us into his family so we might be sent out to our neighbors

Summary

This sermon launches a series on community and service, teaching from Titus 2 that God's grace adopts believers into a family who are called to live in intentional community with one another and sacrificially serve their neighbors. The pastor emphasizes that Christian maturity is not just knowing information but practicing obedience through relationships and service.

Key points

  • Adoption: God's grace brings us into his family
  • Alignment: our identity as God's children motivates obedience to say no to sin and yes to service
  • Anticipation: waiting for Christ's return shapes how we live now
  • Community and service are inseparable practices
  • Practical call to join small groups and serve those in need

Titus 2:11-15Matthew 12:50
