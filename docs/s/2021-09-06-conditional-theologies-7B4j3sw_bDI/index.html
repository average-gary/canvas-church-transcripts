Conditional Theologies

Date2021-09-06
Length00:44:45
Words8,310
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

Deconstruction often stems from false conditional theologies that promise blessing for either following desires or following rules, when the true gospel requires only worshiping God and serving Him alone.

Summary

This sermon examines how false 'if-then' beliefs about God contribute to faith deconstruction. Pastor Ryan argues that both the rebellious heart ('if it feels good, pursue it') and the religious heart ('if I obey rules, God must bless me') operate on conditional theologies God never promised, leading to anger and disillusionment when expectations fail.

Key points

  • People build faith on false conditional statements: rebellion says pursue whatever feels good without limits
  • Religion creates man-made rules and expects God to bless obedience to them
  • Adam and Eve illustrate rebellious theology; Cain illustrates religious theology
  • Both approaches lead to anger at God when the expected outcomes don't materialize
  • Jesus models the true way: worship the Lord your God and serve Him only
