Big idea

The church demonstrates spiritual maturity and gospel witness by choosing reconciliation over division when conflicts arise, staying unified around Jesus even through significant disagreements.

Summary

This sermon addresses how Christians should respond to conflict and disagreement within the church community. In contrast to cancel culture's tendency toward division and outrage, believers are called to pursue reconciliation and unity as markers of spiritual maturity. The preacher argues that staying together through difficult conflicts rather than splitting apart is both a witness to the world and essential for spiritual growth.

Key points

  • Division over preferences is a marker of spiritual immaturity, not maturity
  • Unity requires humility, gentleness, patience, and sacrificial love from both parties
  • The cycle of immaturity: conflict leads to division, uniformity, and isolation
  • The cycle of maturity: conflict leads to reconciliation, unity, and growth
  • People who stay in community grow; those who leave do not
