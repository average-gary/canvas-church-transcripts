Big idea

God speaks to His sheep through many means, but we will only recognize His voice and experience the abundant life He offers if we intentionally draw close to Him and obediently act on what He says.

Summary

This sermon teaches that God wants to speak to His people and guide them into abundant life, but believers must intentionally take time to listen and learn to recognize the Shepherd's voice through intimacy with Him. The greatest barrier to hearing God is not His silence but our failure to listen, and once we hear, we must act in obedience.

Key points

  • God wants to speak to us and lead us into abundant life
  • Listening to God requires intention—we must slow down and tune in
  • We recognize God's voice by knowing Him intimately, not just seeking Him in emergencies
  • God speaks in many ways: intuition, conviction, dreams, Scripture, circumstances, and more
  • Acting on what God says is the most crucial part—obedience over certainty
