Big idea

The broken body of Jesus is the key to understanding both the Scriptures and the Christian life, and the resurrection makes sacrificial obedience worth it.

Summary

This sermon examines the post-resurrection encounter between Jesus and two discouraged disciples on the road to Emmaus (Luke 24). The disciples had lost hope after Jesus' crucifixion, but when the risen Jesus walks with them and explains how the Old Testament pointed to a suffering Messiah, their eyes are opened when he breaks bread with them.

Key points

  • Two disciples walk away from Jerusalem in confusion and disappointment after Jesus' death
  • Jesus joins them unrecognized and explains how Scripture predicted a suffering Messiah, not a political conqueror
  • Their eyes are opened when Jesus breaks bread, revealing his identity
  • They immediately repent and return to Jerusalem
  • The Christian life follows the same pattern: victory through sacrificial love and self-denial
