Kindness Leads to Repentance

Date2022-07-11
Length00:41:38
Words7,279
SeriesFruit of the Spirit
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

God's kindness shown in Christ should transform us into people who actively seek others' good, using kindness as powerful resistance against the world's cruelty.

Summary

This sermon explores kindness as a fruit of the Spirit, arguing that biblical kindness goes far beyond being nice—it is actively seeking what is good and beneficial for others, flowing from love. The pastor emphasizes that God's kindness, especially demonstrated through Christ's sacrifice, should transform believers into people who treat kindness not as optional niceness but as a core virtue and powerful act of resistance against evil.

Key points

  • Biblical kindness means actively seeking what is good and beneficial for others, not just being nice
  • God's fundamental character is kind; we have received His kindness through salvation in Christ
  • Kindness should be our default disposition toward everyone, not earned privilege
  • Kindness is strength that exposes evil, not weakness
  • Kindness is a revolutionary act of resistance in a cruel and divided world
