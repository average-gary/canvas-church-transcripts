Big idea

Christians are called to cultivate God's kingdom on earth by both proclaiming Jesus clearly through spiritual conversations and obeying Jesus boldly through acts of service and radical hospitality.

Summary

This sermon unpacks the church's vision for fulfilling the external aspect of the Great Commission—going out into the world to make disciples. Pastor Ryan addresses the common tension Christians feel about evangelism and presents a holistic approach that involves both proclaiming Jesus clearly through spiritual conversations and obeying Jesus boldly through meeting the needs of others, following Jesus's model of radical hospitality.

Key points

  • Cultivate the kingdom of God by participating in what God is already doing—the harvest is ready, workers are needed
  • Evangelism is not about recruiting converts but inviting people into God's kingdom on earth as it is in heaven
  • Make room in busy schedules for spiritual conversations by being available and attentive to the Holy Spirit's prompting
  • Demonstrate the kingdom through bold obedience—caring for orphans, widows, and those in need
  • Follow Jesus's example of radical hospitality by making space at the table for those the world has rejected
