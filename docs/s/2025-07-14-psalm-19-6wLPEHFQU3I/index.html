Big idea

God communicates His wisdom, power, and beauty through creation and His word, and we mature in relationship with Him by learning to receive His love in the ways He has chosen to reveal Himself, allowing that revelation to lead us to repentance.

Summary

This sermon on Psalm 19 explores how God communicates His love and character to humanity through three primary languages: creation, which reveals His wisdom, power, and beauty to all people; His written word, which provides clearer revelation and is good, enlightening, encouraging, and convicting; and self-reflection, where God's revelation should lead us to recognize hidden sins and repent of willful ones.

Key points

  • Creation universally declares God's wisdom, power, and beauty without words
  • God's written word draws closer and gives clearer definition of His character as compassionate, gracious, and abounding in steadfast love
  • When we encounter difficult passages in Scripture, we should wrestle with them while trusting God's revealed character
  • God's revelation through creation and word should lead to self-reflection and repentance
  • We must learn to receive God's love in the ways He has chosen to communicate it
