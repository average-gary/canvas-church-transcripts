Big idea

Building your life on faith in and obedience to Jesus's teachings, not merely intellectual agreement, is what enables you to persevere through any storm, including death itself.

Summary

This sermon on Matthew 7:24-29 contrasts hearing Jesus's teachings versus obeying them, challenging the audience to move beyond intellectual agreement to active obedience. The pastor explores the parable of the wise and foolish builders, examining different types of storms believers face and calling for rededication to building life on the rock of faith in and submission to Christ.

Key points

  • Information without application leads to stagnation; both builders heard Jesus's words but only one obeyed
  • Jesus speaks with divine authority, calling us to submit to His lordship, not just admire His moral teachings
  • Storms come in three forms: individual storms from our own decisions, storms caused by others, and the cosmic storm of death
  • Jesus is the rock who weathered the ultimate storm and rose from death, proving obedience to Him brings perseverance
  • A call to rededication for those who have drifted from building their lives on Christ
