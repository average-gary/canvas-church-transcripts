Big idea

God's ultimate purpose in every season of spiritual formation—even the most difficult ones—is to transform our grief into inexpressible joy so that we glorify Him by enjoying Him forever.

Summary

The sermon addresses how God uses various seasons of spiritual formation—including difficult ones—to ultimately bring believers to greater joy and Christ-likeness. The preacher emphasizes that no matter what season you're in, God's end goal is your enjoyment and that your grief will be transformed into joy.

Key points

  • God uses seasons of formation to build resilience and make us more like Jesus, regardless of how we entered that season
  • The end goal of all God's seasons is our enjoyment—we glorify God by enjoying Him forever
  • Every resurrection is preceded by a death; knowing Christ's resurrection power requires fellowship with His sufferings
  • Grief will not just be replaced by joy but transformed into it, like labor pains that bring forth new life
  • Whatever season you're in now, God has a next season planned—trust His goodness
