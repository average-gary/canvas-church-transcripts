Big idea

True blessing comes not from hiding our sin but from confessing it to God, who faithfully forgives and transforms our shame into past history rather than a burden that defines our future.

Summary

This sermon on Psalm 32 explores the blessing that comes from confessing sin to God rather than hiding it. The preacher walks through David's experience of spiritual and physical distress when concealing sin, contrasted with the freedom, joy, and restoration that follow genuine confession and God's forgiveness.

Key points

  • Hiding sin leads to spiritual dryness and oppression; confession brings freedom and abundance
  • God covers (atones for) our sin when we stop covering it up ourselves
  • Genuine confession requires honesty before God and trusted believers, not self-deception
  • We must confess while there is still time, before the final judgment
  • Forgiveness itself is the blessing, leading to ongoing gratitude and joy
