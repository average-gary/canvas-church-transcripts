Like No Other: Healing the Blind Man

Date2023-10-23
Length00:46:49
Words7,360

Big idea

Jesus heals physical blindness to expose the spiritual blindness caused by bad theology, cultural preferences, and fear, inviting us to see God's miraculous work and use our testimony to help others find sight.

Summary

This sermon examines John 9, where Jesus heals a man born blind, using the miracle as a lived-out parable exposing spiritual blindness. The pastor shows how the disciples, neighbors, Pharisees, and parents all missed the supernatural work of God because they were blinded by bad theology, fear, cultural preferences, and religious tradition.

Key points

  • The disciples prioritized theological debate over the suffering person in front of them
  • The neighbors and Pharisees were skeptical of the miracle and overly submissive to religious authority
  • The Pharisees mixed cultural ideology with theology and couldn't recognize God's work on the Sabbath
  • The parents feared losing their religious community and social status
  • The healed man repeatedly shared his testimony despite opposition
