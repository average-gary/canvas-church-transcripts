Big idea

The gospel demands exclusive allegiance to Jesus as Lord, which offends both religious and secular culture, and the church must prioritize faithful obedience and integrity over doctrinal correctness while remaining vigilant against false teaching.

Summary

This sermon examines Paul's farewell address to the Ephesian elders in Acts 20, where he summarizes his gospel message—repentance toward God and faith in Jesus as Lord—and explains why it offended both religious Jews and secular Gentiles. Paul warns church leaders to prioritize personal holiness and obedience over doctrinal correctness while guarding against false teachers.

Key points

  • Paul's core message: repentance toward God and faith in Jesus alone as Lord
  • Jews were offended by pride and fear of losing religious privilege; Gentiles resisted exclusive allegiance to Jesus
  • Church leaders must prioritize personal faithfulness and integrity before ministry tasks
  • Obedience and relationship with God matter more than perfect doctrine
  • The church was purchased with Christ's blood and requires faithful stewardship
