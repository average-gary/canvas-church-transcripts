Big idea

The dragon counterfeits God's kingdom to compete for our worship and allegiance, but we conquer by following the Lamb wherever he goes.

Summary

This sermon examines Revelation 13-14, teaching that Satan creates counterfeit kingdoms (the 'unholy trinity') to deceive believers into giving their worship and allegiance to temporary earthly powers rather than to Jesus. The dragon imitates God's kingdom through political empires, cultural systems, and personal idols, using both deception and coercion to steal worship from the true King.

Key points

  • The dragon counterfeits God's kingdom through an unholy trinity: the dragon (Father), beast of the sea (Jesus), and beast of the earth (Holy Spirit)
  • Counterfeit kingdoms deceive through imitation and coerce through persecution, promising temporary safety and prosperity
  • The mark of the beast symbolizes spiritual allegiance, not merely a physical identifier
  • Counterfeit kingdoms include not only political empires but also career, entertainment, nationalism, and personal ambitions
  • Victory comes through following the Lamb wherever he goes with total surrender and allegiance
