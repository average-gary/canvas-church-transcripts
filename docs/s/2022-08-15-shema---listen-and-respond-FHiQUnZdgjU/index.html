Shema - Listen and Respond

Date2022-08-15
Length00:34:29
Words5,209
VideoYouTube ↗

Big idea

True faith means not only hearing God's command to love Him fully and love our neighbors, but actively responding through obedient action in daily life.

Summary

This sermon explores the Hebrew concept of 'Shema' from Deuteronomy 6, teaching that listening to God means both hearing and responding through action. The pastor connects this to Jesus' teaching on the greatest commandment—loving God with everything you are and loving your neighbor as yourself—showing that genuine faith requires obedient response, not just intellectual agreement.

Key points

  • Shema means listening AND responding, not merely hearing
  • The greatest commandment: love God fully and love your neighbor as yourself
  • Everyone is your neighbor regardless of differences
  • True listening to God requires changed actions
  • The gospel says we're accepted, therefore we obey
