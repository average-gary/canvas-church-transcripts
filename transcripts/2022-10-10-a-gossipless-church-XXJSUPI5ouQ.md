# A Gossipless Church

*2022-10-10 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXJSUPI5ouQ) — 00:43:08*

> **True church unity requires addressing gossip at its root by examining our heart motivations and following Jesus' example of refusing retaliation, speaking truth in love, and leaving justice to God.**

## Summary

This sermon addresses gossip and slander as primary threats to church unity, particularly timely as two congregations merge. Ryan defines gossip as bearing bad news behind someone's back out of a bad heart, and explains that stopping gossip requires addressing heart motivations—insecurity, revenge, fear of man, and boredom—rather than merely controlling behavior.

**Key points**

- Gossip destroys unity and fuels anger by creating echo chambers of one-sided perspectives
- Gossip is bearing bad news (false, shameful, or partial truth) behind someone's back from a bad heart
- Both speaking and listening to gossip are equally damaging to relationships and community
- Root motivations include insecurity, revenge, fear of man, and boredom
- Jesus is the perfect example of refusing retaliation and leaving justice to God
