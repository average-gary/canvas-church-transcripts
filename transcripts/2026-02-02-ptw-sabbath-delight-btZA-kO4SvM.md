# PTW Sabbath: DELIGHT

*2026-02-02 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=btZA-kO4SvM) — 00:46:11*

> **Sabbath is God's gift to reorient us from our natural bent toward worry and negativity by creating intentional space to see His goodness in the world and trust that He provides everything we need.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Sabbath as a practice of delight that reorients our perspective from scarcity and worry toward seeing God's goodness and abundance in the world. Brian shares how his family has practiced Sabbath for 3-4 years, offering practical guidance on cultivating a 'good eye' that recognizes God's provision and the inherent goodness of creation.

**Key points**

- God created the world, called it 'good' (tov), and rested on the seventh day
- Cultivating a 'good eye' (ayin tovah) sees abundance and bears God's image versus a 'bad eye' that sees scarcity
- Sabbath helps redirect our natural negativity (70-80% of thoughts) toward intentional gratitude and delight
- Practical rhythms include preparing ahead, marking boundaries with rituals, putting away worry, and doing joy-filled activities
- Sabbath in community multiplies the experience and counter-culturally positions Christians as hope-bringers

**Scripture:** Genesis 1, Genesis 2:1-3, Matthew 6:22-23, Matthew 6:25-34
