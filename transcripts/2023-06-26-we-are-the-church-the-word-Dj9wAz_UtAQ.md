# We Are the Church: The Word

*2023-06-26 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dj9wAz_UtAQ) — 00:51:21*

> **The church requires members to accept the Bible as God's authoritative Word because Scripture reveals Jesus Christ (the living Word) and serves as the foundation for both right belief and right living while spiritually forming us through the Holy Spirit's work.**

## Summary

This sermon explains why the church requires members to accept the Bible as God's Word, emphasizing that Jesus Christ is the living Word to whom all Scripture points. The pastor unpacks how to properly understand and rely on both the written Word (Scripture) and the living Word (Jesus), and how the Bible teaches, corrects, and spiritually forms believers.

**Key points**

- Jesus Christ is the supreme revelation—the 'living Word' to whom all Scripture points
- The Bible progressively reveals God's single plan of salvation from Old Testament to New Testament
- Scripture is both fully human and fully divine, inspired and authoritative
- The Bible is profitable for teaching right beliefs, rebuking wrong beliefs, correcting behavior, and training in righteousness
- The Word of God is meant to form us spiritually as we sit before it and let the Holy Spirit reveal our 'unknown unknowns'

**Scripture:** John 14, 2 Timothy 3:14-17, Romans 10:14-17, Psalm 119, Romans 7, Psalm 91:4, Hebrews
