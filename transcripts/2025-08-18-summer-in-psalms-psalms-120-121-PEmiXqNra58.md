# Summer in Psalms: PSALMS 120-121

*2025-08-18 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PEmiXqNra58) — 00:36:28*

> **The Christian life is a pilgrimage where God shapes us along the journey through community, hardship, and perseverance, not just at the final destination, and we must trust Him and walk together every step of the way.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces the Psalms of Ascent (120-134), ancient traveling songs sung by pilgrims journeying to Jerusalem, and applies them to the Christian life as a communal pilgrimage. The preacher contrasts being a tourist (focused on sights and comfort) with being a pilgrim (focused on transformation and discovery), arguing that discipleship happens along the road, not just at the destination.

**Key points**

- Psalms 120-134 were worship songs for pilgrims traveling to Jerusalem for feasts
- The Christian life is a pilgrimage, not a destination—God shapes us on the journey
- Psalm 120 begins with crying out in distress, the first step of discipleship
- Discipleship is communal; we need traveling companions and cannot journey alone
- Psalm 121 lifts eyes to God who keeps and watches over us on the road
- Spiritual growth requires slow, costly perseverance, not microwavable Christ-likeness

**Scripture:** Psalm 120, Psalm 121, Genesis (Meshach and Kedar references), Hebrews (foreigners and strangers)
