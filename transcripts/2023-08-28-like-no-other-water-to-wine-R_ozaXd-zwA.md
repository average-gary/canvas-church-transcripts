# Like No Other: Water to Wine

*2023-08-28 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_ozaXd-zwA) — 00:44:08*

> **Jesus drank our cup of suffering so that we can drink his cup of celebration, providing the perfect sacrifice we could not offer and securing both abundant life now and eternal life in the future.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Jesus' first miracle at the wedding in Cana, where he turned water into wine. The preacher argues that John presents both a surface narrative and deeper symbolic layers that reveal Jesus as Messiah who fulfills the old covenant through his sacrificial blood and inaugurates a new covenant of blessing and celebration.

**Key points**

- Running out of wine at a Jewish wedding was a catastrophic social and legal failure
- Jesus used purification jars from the old covenant system to perform the miracle
- Water turned to wine twice: in the jars (representing Christ's blood/sacrifice) and in the glass (representing future blessing)
- Jesus provided what the groom failed to provide, picturing how he fulfills obligations humanity cannot meet
- The miracle demonstrates Jesus' divine power over nature, time, and his ability to create

**Scripture:** John 2:1-11, John 1, Romans 3, Romans 8:3-4, Luke 22:20, Amos 9
