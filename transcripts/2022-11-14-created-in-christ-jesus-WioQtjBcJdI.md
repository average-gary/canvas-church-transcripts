# Created in Christ Jesus

*2022-11-14 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WioQtjBcJdI) — 00:36:12*

> **God created us in Christ Jesus not to make us whole by removing our brokenness, but to enter our places of pain and transform them into places where His presence dwells and works.**

## Summary

This sermon explores how trauma and wounding shape us from childhood, but God made us with 'elastic brains' capable of transformation. Using Ephesians 2:10-22, the pastor explains that being 'created in Christ Jesus' means God doesn't remove our brokenness but enters into it, turning our places of pain into 'docking stations' for Christ's presence and transforming power.

**Key points**

- Trauma creates neurological pathways that drive behavior more than theology
- God made us with elastic brains capable of rewiring through Christ
- Christ broke down the dividing wall between Jews and Gentiles, creating one new person
- Our brokenness becomes a docking station for Christ, not something to be removed
- We are loved as we are, yet called forward to become more like Christ

**Scripture:** Ephesians 2:10-22, Romans 7, Philippians 3:9, Philippians 3:12, Galatians 3:28
