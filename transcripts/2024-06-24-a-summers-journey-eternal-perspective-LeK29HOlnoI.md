# A SUMMER'S JOURNEY: Eternal Perspective

*2024-06-24 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LeK29HOlnoI) — 00:51:51*

> **Christians are called to live missionally in every circumstance, viewing obstacles and imprisonment not as barriers but as divine opportunities to spread the gospel to those God has prepared to hear it.**

## Summary

This sermon examines Paul's final journey in Acts 21-28, which involved imprisonment, trials, beatings, shipwreck, and eventual arrival in Rome. The speaker argues that Paul lived 'missionally,' viewing his chains not as obstacles but as God-given opportunities to witness to captive audiences, including Roman officials and eventually Caesar himself.

**Key points**

- Paul's imprisonment journey was neither missionary nor prison journey, but a missional life following God's call
- Paul saw chains as freedom to witness to legally obliged audiences who had to listen
- God doesn't have a mission for us; He has us for His mission
- Living missionally means spreading the gospel in every circumstance, not waiting for ideal conditions
- We should speak to those who are listening, as the Moravians and Brother Andrew exemplified

**Scripture:** Matthew 6, Acts 20:21, Acts 21-28, Acts 21:28, Acts 28:28, 2 Corinthians 11, 2 Timothy, Isaiah (prophecy about hardened hearts), Jonah
