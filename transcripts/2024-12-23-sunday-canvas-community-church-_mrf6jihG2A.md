# Sunday @ Canvas Community Church

*2024-12-23 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mrf6jihG2A) — 01:20:31*

> **True peace is not something we achieve by our own efforts but something we receive when we submit to Jesus as our Good Shepherd and follow him even when it doesn't make sense.**

## Summary

This Advent sermon explores biblical peace through the imagery of Jesus as the Good Shepherd, contrasting temporary earthly peace achieved through fighting or avoiding conflict with lasting biblical peace received by submitting to Christ's leadership. The pastor connects messianic prophecies about David to Jesus' role as both King and Shepherd who leads his people into shalom—wholeness and lacking nothing.

**Key points**

- Biblical peace (shalom) means wholeness and completeness, not just absence of conflict
- Earthly peace is temporary, achieved through aggression or avoidance
- Biblical peace is lasting, received through submission and obedience to Jesus as Good Shepherd
- Like sheep without a shepherd, people without Christ live in constant survival mode
- Trusting the Good Shepherd means we don't have to fight enemies or avoid trials

**Scripture:** Isaiah 9:6, Ezekiel 34:11-12, Psalm 23, Luke 2:14
