# NAMES OF GOD: An Introduction

*2025-11-24 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCPqrzcjI3s) — 00:38:53*

> **God reveals his character through his names throughout Scripture to invite us into covenant relationship, ultimately fulfilled in Jesus who embodies God's never-stopping, never-giving-up love and absorbs our covenant-breaking sins so we can dwell with him forever.**

## Summary

This introductory sermon to an Advent series explores how God reveals his character through his names in Scripture, focusing on Exodus 34 where God reveals himself to Moses as merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love, and faithful. The sermon traces how God's covenant name Yahweh ('I will be with you') culminates in Jesus (Yeshua, 'Yahweh is salvation'), who fulfills God's promise to absorb the consequences of our broken covenant and tabernacle among us.

**Key points**

- In Hebrew culture, names convey character and identity; God reveals himself through multiple names depending on context
- Yahweh (YHWH, 'I AM') is God's personal covenant name meaning 'I will be with you'
- God's character revealed to Moses includes mercy, grace, patience, chesed (never-stopping love), faithfulness, forgiveness, and justice
- Despite Israel's immediate covenant-breaking with the golden calf, God demonstrates his commitment to absorb their sin and preserve the relationship
- Jesus embodies God's names and character, taking our covenant debt upon himself so we can remain in relationship with God
