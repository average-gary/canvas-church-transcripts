# EPHESIANS: Radical Submission

*2026-03-23 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2-dJpOXooKE) — 00:41:49*

> **The church transforms the world by forming Christ-like, counter-cultural communities characterized by mutual submission and sacrificial love, living with a foot in both the present world and God's coming kingdom.**

## Summary

This sermon examines the household codes in Ephesians 5-6, arguing that Paul was not prescribing timeless family structures but subverting first-century cultural norms to demonstrate Spirit-filled, mutually submissive relationships. The most radical command is that believers submit to one another out of reverence for Christ, which challenged both ancient honor-shame culture and modern individualism.

**Key points**

- Mutual submission (Eph 5:21) is the core command, challenging both ancient and modern cultural values
- Paul flips secular household codes upside down by addressing subordinates first and insisting relationships are mutually beneficial
- Paul elevates those without cultural power, redefining authority according to Christ's self-sacrificial example
- The passage is ultimately about the church as the working model of new creation, not just nuclear families
- The church transforms the world through counter-cultural community, not through political power
