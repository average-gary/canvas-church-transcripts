# JONAH: Self-Righteousness & Salvation

*2026-05-11 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iYVlnDu9y1k) — 00:42:52*

> **Salvation belongs to God alone, who extends compassionate grace both to the self-righteous religious and to those deemed unworthy, calling all to humble repentance rather than self-justification.**

## Summary

This sermon examines Jonah chapter 2, arguing that the book is not about a whale but about self-righteousness—how we think we deserve God's grace while deeming others unworthy. The preacher draws parallels to modern social media algorithms that reinforce tribal thinking and confronts both the self-righteous religious person and the wayward sinner with God's patient, compassionate grace.

**Key points**

- Self-righteousness distorts reality, leading us to condemn others, accuse God, and justify ourselves
- Jonah did not pray until after God saved him, then used religious language to justify himself without true repentance
- Salvation belongs to the Lord alone and is freely given to all who humbly repent
- God is patient with both the self-righteous prophet and the wicked Ninevites
- The parable of the prodigal son mirrors Jonah's story—both older and younger brothers need the Father's grace

**Scripture:** Jonah 1:17-2:10, Luke 18:9-14, Luke 15:11-32, Matthew 5
