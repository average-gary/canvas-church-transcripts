# PTW COMMUNITY: Be Family Around a Table

*2025-09-15 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AVaF5ujWtKk) — 00:44:45*

> **Christians must resist cultural isolation by intentionally gathering in small, embodied communities centered on Christ's broken body and shed blood at the communion table.**

## Summary

This sermon calls Christians to resist cultural isolation and digital tribalism by prioritizing embodied, face-to-face community centered around the communion table. The pastor argues that while humans were created for intimate community in God's image, sin and modern culture—especially social media and the idealization of the nuclear family—have pulled us out of tune with that design, requiring intentional small-group relationships anchored in Christ's broken body and shed blood.

**Key points**

- We over-invest in digital relationships with strangers while neglecting embodied community
- Political forces have discipled us to dehumanize those we disagree with
- God created humanity for community, imaging the relational Trinity
- Modern obstacles like social media and nuclear-family idealization isolate us from biblical community
- Jesus modeled investing in different sized communities and invites us to the communion table

**Scripture:** Genesis 1:26-28, Genesis 3, Matthew 26, Acts 2
