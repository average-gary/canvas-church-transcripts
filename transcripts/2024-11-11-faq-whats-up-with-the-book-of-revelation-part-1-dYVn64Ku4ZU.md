# FAQ: What's up with the Book of Revelation? Part 1

*2024-11-11 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dYVn64Ku4ZU) — 00:45:59*

> **God is seated on His throne in heaven, and Jesus the slain Lamb has secured victory through sacrificial love—a reality that should encourage believers to trust God and follow the way of the Lamb regardless of earthly circumstances.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces the Book of Revelation as a message meant to encourage first-century believers being persecuted by Rome, not primarily to predict the future. Through Revelation 4-5, the pastor unpacks the vision of God's throne room in heaven, revealing that Jesus the Lamb is seated on the throne and has secured victory through sacrificial love.

**Key points**

- Revelation was written to first-century Christians, not primarily for us to predict modern end-times events
- The symbols and numbers were clear to the original audience who knew apocalyptic literature and Old Testament references
- God is seated on His throne in heaven—the true reality behind earthly suffering
- Jesus the Lamb (not worldly power) is worthy to open the scroll and bring about redemption
- Believers are called to respond through worship and participate as a kingdom of priests through sacrificial love

**Scripture:** Revelation 1:1, Acts 1:6-7, Revelation 4:1-2, Revelation 4:8-11, Revelation 5:1-14
