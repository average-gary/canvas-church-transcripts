# A Unified Church

*2022-10-03 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s76eap9thvI) — 00:42:06*

> **Individual spiritual maturity, achieved by fixing our eyes on Jesus who perfectly balances truth and love, leads to genuine church unity.**

## Summary

This sermon teaches that church unity is built on individual spiritual maturity, which comes from fixing our eyes on Jesus who perfectly balances truth and love. The preacher warns against two false teachings that distract believers: religion (truth without love) and postmodernism (love without truth), both of which prevent genuine spiritual growth and unity.

**Key points**

- Individual spiritual maturity leads to corporate church unity
- Spiritual maturity means obedience to Jesus, not just intellectual knowledge
- Two false teachings distract from maturity: truth without love and love without truth
- Jesus is the perfect balance of truth and love, displayed at the cross
- As individuals become like Jesus, the church becomes unified and reflects Christ

**Scripture:** Ephesians 4:11-16, Romans 8:29, Ephesians 2:10, Revelation 2:1-7, 1 Corinthians 13, Hebrews 12:1-2
