# Poured Out: Seat-Belt Christians

*2023-06-05 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jaWKgiHCgOQ) — 00:39:02*

> **Christians must lay aside concern for their own reputation and embrace vulnerability and confession, following Jesus who made himself of no reputation, so the world sees Christ rather than self-righteous stumbling blocks.**

## Summary

This sermon challenges Christians to follow Jesus' example of having 'no reputation' by prioritizing vulnerability and authenticity over protecting their image. The preacher argues that the church's self-righteousness and reputation-management often becomes the biggest stumbling block preventing the world from seeing Jesus, and calls for confession, repentance, and genuine community accountability instead.

**Key points**

- The church can either further God's kingdom or be its biggest stumbling block
- Jesus made himself of no reputation, taking the form of a servant
- Protecting our reputation leads to hiding sin and self-righteous hypocrisy
- We should build Jesus' reputation, not our own or our church's
- Authentic community requires vulnerability, confession, and mutual accountability

**Scripture:** Philippians 2:5-7, Titus 1:6, 1 Timothy 3:2, Isaiah 53, Galatians 5
