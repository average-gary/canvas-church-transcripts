# EPHESIANS: On Earth as it is in Heaven

*2026-02-16 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Oa8O4q11h4) — 00:37:31*

> **God's kingdom grows on earth as believers understand their adopted identity as heirs with full inheritance rights, which naturally produces kingdom behavior rather than requiring obedience through willpower.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces a six-week series on Ephesians, framing it as Paul's explanation to Gentile believers of how God's kingdom operates on earth as it is in heaven. The letter describes how Gentiles are adopted into God's kingdom with full inheritance rights alongside Jewish believers, and how transformed behavior flows naturally from understanding one's identity in Christ rather than from striving to obey commands.

**Key points**

- The Lord's Prayer describes kingdom life: provision without scarcity, forgiveness flowing outward, freedom from temptation and evil
- Paul writes to Gentile believers explaining their adoption as sons with full inheritance and power through Christ
- The first half of Ephesians establishes identity; the second half describes what naturally follows when living in that identity
- The kingdom of God is present now but not fully visible - it grows organically, invading injustice person by person
- Behavior flows from identity, not vice versa; when struggling with the imperatives, return to understanding who you are in Christ
