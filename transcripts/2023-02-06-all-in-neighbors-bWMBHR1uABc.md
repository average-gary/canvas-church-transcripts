> **Christians are called to partner with God's work by living transformed lives that draw others to Jesus and sharing authentic stories of how He satisfies their deepest needs, rather than relying on guilt-driven formulas or outdated methods.**

## Summary

This sermon challenges Christians to share their faith with neighbors, friends, and coworkers by living compelling lives and telling authentic stories rather than relying on guilt-driven or outdated evangelism methods. Using Jesus's encounter with the Samaritan woman, the pastor argues that the gospel offers abundant life now—not just heaven later—and that believers should demonstrate this transformation in ways that make others curious about faith.

**Key points**

- Overcome spiritual, cultural, and personal obstacles to evangelism
- Recognize people's spiritual thirst that only Jesus can satisfy
- Update evangelism methodology and reclaim the full gospel message
- Live compelling lives that make others wish Christianity were true
- Tell personal stories of transformation rather than using guilt tactics

**Scripture:** Acts 1:8, John 4:4-26, Ecclesiastes 3:11, Romans 1:16, 1 Corinthians 3:6-8
