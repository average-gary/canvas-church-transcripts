# CENTER OF THE UNIVERSE?

*2022-04-25 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uK_k9c-ScWU) — 00:46:48*

> **True freedom and flourishing come not from trying to control our lives and judge others, but from humbly submitting to God's will and obeying the royal law to love God and neighbor.**

## Summary

This sermon on James 4:11-17 confronts the 'God complex' many Christians have, living as though they are the center of the universe. The preacher argues that this prideful posture is revealed in two specific behaviors: speaking evil against others with a judgmental heart and making plans for the future with a prideful heart that assumes control.

**Key points**

- Never speak evil against fellow believers; our job is to obey God's law, not judge others by it
- We demonstrate a God complex by treating others judgmentally rather than focusing on our own obedience
- We make plans out of pride, trying to control outcomes rather than holding plans with open hands
- Life is like vapor—we have no control over tomorrow and must trust God as provider
- Jesus modeled perfect humility by not grasping equality with God but submitting to death on a cross

**Scripture:** James 4:10, James 4:11-12, James 4:13-17, Matthew 7:5, Matthew 6:25-27, Philippians 2:3-11
