# RADICAL LIFE: Kingdom Maturity

*2026-01-12 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JVqPjr8nq0A) — 00:43:50*

> **Spiritual maturity means identifying where our culture's obsession with success and power contradicts the kingdom values of Christ crucified, and choosing to trust him at the bottom of the worldly ladder rather than climbing it.**

## Summary

This sermon challenges believers to mature by recognizing where Western cultural values of success, power, and influence contradict kingdom values exemplified in Christ crucified. Using 1 Corinthians 1, the pastor argues that the Corinthian church blended their culture with the gospel—just as American Christians often do—creating a 'semi-Christian worldview' that seeks upward mobility rather than sacrificial love.

**Key points**

- Christians often reflect surrounding culture, creating 'oxymoronic' semi-Christian worldviews
- Corinthian culture valued climbing social ladders through power and wisdom, similar to modern Western culture
- Christ crucified represents God's true power (sacrificial love) and wisdom (love of enemies), contradicting cultural values
- Maturity requires identifying, examining, and redefining values based on the kingdom rather than culture
- Parents must consider what values they impart to children—kingdom faithfulness or cultural success

**Scripture:** 1 Corinthians 1:18-25, Matthew 5:3-12, Romans 8
