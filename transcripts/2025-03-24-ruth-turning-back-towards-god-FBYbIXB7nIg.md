# RUTH: Turning Back Towards God

*2025-03-24 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FBYbIXB7nIg) — 00:44:06*

> **True repentance requires completely abandoning the old life and committing energetically to following God through suffering, trusting Him even when the path seems irrational and offers no material prosperity.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Ruth chapter 1 as a story of suffering and repentance, contrasting Naomi's bitterness with Ruth's radical decision to leave her Moabite background and pagan gods to follow Yahweh. The preacher emphasizes that true repentance means burning bridges with the old life and committing fully to God, even when it seems irrational.

**Key points**

- Naomi embodies comprehensive suffering: loss of husband, sons, homeland, and hope
- Ruth makes an unreasonable but faithful choice to cling to Naomi and Yahweh
- True repentance requires dying to the old life and energetically pursuing righteousness
- Life grows from ashes (Ephratah) when we turn faithfully toward God
- Small acts of faithful obedience can profoundly impact history
