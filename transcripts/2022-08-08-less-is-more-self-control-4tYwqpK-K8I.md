# Less is More: Self Control

*2022-08-08 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4tYwqpK-K8I) — 00:46:33*

> **True spiritual power is found in exercising self-control over our own thoughts and actions rather than trying to control external circumstances, and we must remove spiritual clutter from our lives to walk faithfully with Christ and experience the abundant life he promises.**

## Summary

This sermon explores self-control as a fruit of the Spirit, arguing that true power comes from controlling oneself rather than external circumstances. Using David's restraint in sparing Saul's life (1 Samuel 24) as the central example, the speaker emphasizes that believers must remove spiritual clutter—sin, toxic patterns, and baggage—to experience the abundant life Christ offers and to walk faithfully until his return.

**Key points**

- Real power is the ability to control yourself, not others or circumstances
- Spiritual clutter (sin, unforgiveness, bad habits) prevents us from seeing and experiencing God's presence
- David demonstrated self-control by refusing to kill Saul despite having the opportunity and his men's encouragement
- Christ is the ultimate example of self-control, humbling himself and choosing the cross for our sake
- Believers must prune fruitless behaviors and cultivate self-discipline to produce spiritual fruit and live abundantly
