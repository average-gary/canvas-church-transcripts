# RADICAL LIFE: Spiritual Maturity

*2026-01-05 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QVwwUT0dHTw) — 00:49:09*

> **The church grows both numerically and spiritually when people are welcomed as they are and invited to live by kingdom values rather than cultural ones.**

## Summary

This sermon examines how the early church in Acts achieved both numerical growth and spiritual maturity by removing unnecessary cultural barriers for outsiders while calling all believers to embrace kingdom values over cultural values. The pastor challenges Canvas Community Church to welcome seekers as they are while inviting everyone to surrender American cultural idols like individualism and materialism for Christ-centered living, particularly sacrificial enemy love.

**Key points**

- The church must hold in tension both welcoming outsiders and maturing insiders
- Remove unnecessary barriers for those exploring faith, as the Jerusalem Council did by not requiring circumcision
- Call people to leave behind cultural values (individualism, materialism) for kingdom values, even when it seems foolish
- Challenge insiders to avoid blending nationalism or moralism with authentic Christianity
- Paul having Timothy circumcised demonstrates laying down freedom for the sake of reaching enemies with the gospel

**Scripture:** Acts 16:5, Acts 16:1-4, Acts 15:23-29, Matthew 5-7, 1 Corinthians 1:18-25
