# Prophecy Points

*2021-11-01 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oq5U9XyqygI) — 00:47:58*

> **All biblical prophecy either points to the first coming of Jesus or prepares believers for his second coming, and this truth should inspire hope rather than fear or obsessive speculation.**

## Summary

This sermon explores how to read biblical prophecy through the lens of Jesus's two comings, using Daniel 7 as a case study. The pastor interprets the four beasts as kingdoms (Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome) that God sovereignly used to prepare history for Christ's first arrival, then addresses the Antichrist and the return of Christ.

**Key points**

- All prophecy points to the first coming of Jesus or prepares us for his second coming
- Overconfidence in eschatology can distract from our mission to preach the gospel
- God sovereignly works through secular kingdoms to accomplish his purposes
- The spirit of the Antichrist works in every generation to deceive people
- Prophecy should inspire hope, not fear

**Scripture:** Daniel 7, 1 Peter 1:10-12, Matthew 24:3-8, 14, 1 John 2:18
