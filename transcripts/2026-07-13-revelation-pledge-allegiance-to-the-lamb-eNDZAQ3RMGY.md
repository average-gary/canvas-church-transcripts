## Summary

This sermon on Revelation 4-5 presents these chapters as the interpretive center of Revelation, revealing that Jesus the Lamb already sits enthroned through his sacrificial death and resurrection. The crisis facing the church is not whether Jesus might lose the throne, but whether believers will remember who sits on it and follow his way of conquering through faithful witness and sacrificial love rather than the empire's methods of power and violence.

- The throne is occupied—God reigns now, not someday
- John hears 'lion' but sees a 'slain lamb'—victory through sacrificial love
- Worship participates in the heavenly reality where Christ's reign is already recognized
- The church conquers by following the Lamb's pattern of faithful witness, even unto suffering
- Communion is a pledge of allegiance to Christ above all earthly powers
