# THE DIGITAL FAST: A Digital Rule of Life

*2026-06-22 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hRVT8IuAGW8) — 00:49:44*

> **If we do not intentionally rehearse God's story in our ordinary daily rhythms—especially around screen time—we and our children will drift into cultural patterns that define good and evil for ourselves rather than surrendering to God's wisdom.**

## Summary

This sermon concludes a Digital Detox series by calling families to create a 'rule of life' around screens—intentional practices and rhythms that help them abide in Christ rather than drift into cultural patterns. Using Deuteronomy 6, the pastor shows how Moses commanded Israel to rehearse God's story in ordinary daily moments (sitting, walking, lying down, rising) so future generations would remember God's goodness and not define good and evil for themselves.

**Key points**

- Craft an intentional rule of life for your screen habits and your family's
- Deuteronomy 6 calls us to rehearse God's story in ordinary daily rhythms, especially with children
- Without intentional practices, we drift and forget God's goodness—like Israel after Joshua
- Parents and spiritual leaders must model surrender and set boundaries around devices
- Screen time consumes the moments Moses lists: relaxing, traveling, bedtime, waking—creating space for God's voice requires intentionality

**Scripture:** Deuteronomy 6:1-17, Romans 12:1-2, Judges 2:7-11, Joshua 24:15, Revelation 4-5
