## Summary

This Advent sermon explores Jesus as the Prince of Peace (Sar Shalom in Hebrew), meaning the ruler of wholeness and completeness. The speaker traces how God's Old Testament promises—from Eden through Noah and Abraham—culminate in Jesus's birth, which restores the broken relationship between humanity and God, offering a peace that transcends worldly circumstances.

**Key points**

- Sar Shalom means ruler of wholeness/completeness, not merely absence of conflict
- Sin separated humanity from God's presence in Eden; Jesus restores that wholeness
- God's covenant promises (rainbow to Noah, blood covenant with Abraham) pointed to Jesus taking our punishment
- Jesus fulfilled over 300 prophecies, proving God's plan was always in motion
- True peace comes from trusting God, grasping his love, and pursuing reconciliation with others
