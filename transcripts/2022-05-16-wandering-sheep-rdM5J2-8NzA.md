# Wandering Sheep

*2022-05-16 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rdM5J2-8NzA) — 00:55:53*

> **God calls every believer to lovingly pursue and restore wandering Christians back to truth, motivated by the same compassion Christ showed us when we were lost.**

## Summary

This sermon on James 5:19-20 addresses believers who wander from the truth and the responsibility of the Christian community to lovingly restore them. Using the metaphor of sheep and the Good Shepherd, the message emphasizes that all Christians are called to watch over one another, confront wandering with compassion rather than judgment, and point each other back to Jesus.

**Key points**

- Believers can wander from truth doctrinally or behaviorally through distraction, idolatry, or sinful patterns
- Jesus as the Good Shepherd leaves the ninety-nine to seek the one lost sheep
- The priesthood of all believers means every Christian has responsibility to restore wandering brothers and sisters
- Restoration requires love, compassion, and humility—not accusation or self-righteousness
- Christian community and serving are essential to prevent isolation and enable accountability
