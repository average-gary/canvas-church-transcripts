# Faithfulness

*2022-08-01 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SpZsMCpjD9c) — 00:53:28*

> **God's unchanging faithfulness to His promises should persuade us to respond with faithful, creative stewardship of the gifts and resources He has given us, without fear of failure.**

## Summary

Two pastors team-teach on faithfulness during a joint church service. Matt Lewis examines God's faithfulness through three key passages, emphasizing that God remains faithful even when circumstances are difficult and that His character is congruent with His actions. Ryan then defines Christian faithfulness as the visible expression of being convinced that God keeps His promises, using the parable of the talents to call believers to creative stewardship of their gifts and resources without fear of failure.

**Key points**

- God's faithfulness is rooted in His unchanging character—who He is matches what He does
- God strengthens and protects His people even when He doesn't remove their problems
- Christian faithfulness flows from being persuaded that God keeps His promises
- The difference between faithful and unfaithful servants is what they believe about their Master's character
- Believers are called to be creative stewards of the gifts and resources God has entrusted to them

**Scripture:** 1 Thessalonians 5:23-24, 2 Thessalonians 3:1-3, 2 Timothy 2:13, Romans 8:28-29, Matthew 25:14-30, Ephesians 2:10, Hebrews 10:23-25
