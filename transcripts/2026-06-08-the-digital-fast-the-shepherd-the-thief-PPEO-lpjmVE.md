# THE DIGITAL FAST: The Shepherd & the Thief

*2026-06-08 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PPEO-lpjmVE) — 00:38:39*

> **Every voice in our digital age promises abundant life, but only Jesus the Good Shepherd truly delivers it, and we must limit competing voices to hear and follow him.**

## Summary

This sermon uses Jesus's teaching in John 10 about the Good Shepherd to address how digital devices and the voices they carry (influencers, algorithms, news feeds, entertainment) function as 'thieves and robbers' that steal our time, consume us for profit, and render our lives useless, while Jesus offers abundant life to those who hear and follow his voice. The message calls the congregation to participate in a 28-day digital fast not as mere religious practice but to create space to recognize and obey the Good Shepherd's voice above the noise.

**Key points**

- Modern technology fills our lives with competing voices that promise life but lead to stealing, killing, and destroying
- Jesus contrasts the thief (who steals, kills, and destroys) with the Good Shepherd (who knows his sheep by name and gives abundant life)
- Tech companies intentionally consume our attention for profit, like cigarette and sugar companies before them
- The core question is whether we actually hear Jesus's voice amid the noise, not just whether we have good theology
- The digital fast aims to restore proximity and relationship with the Good Shepherd through practices like the prayer of examen

**Scripture:** John 10:1-18, Ezekiel 34
