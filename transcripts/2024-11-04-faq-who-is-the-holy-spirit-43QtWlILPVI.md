# FAQ: Who is the Holy Spirit?

*2024-11-04 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=43QtWlILPVI) — 00:49:20*

> **The Holy Spirit is our ever-present advocate and spirit of truth who empowers us to participate in God's work, and we must actively look, listen, learn, and lean in to follow the Spirit's leading.**

## Summary

This sermon explores the identity and role of the Holy Spirit, emphasizing the Spirit as advocate (paraclete) and spirit of truth based on Jesus's teaching in John 14-16. Shauna Rushing calls believers to actively engage with the Holy Spirit's presence and leading through looking, listening, learning, and leaning in with faith and obedience.

**Key points**

- The Holy Spirit is the advocate/helper who comes alongside believers 24/7
- The Holy Spirit is the spirit of truth who testifies to Jesus and guides into all truth
- The Holy Spirit empowers believers through spiritual gifts and fruit
- Practical steps: look for the Spirit's work, listen and slow down, learn from Scripture and others, lean in with faith despite discomfort

**Scripture:** Genesis 1:2, John 3, John 7, John 14-16, Romans 8, Ephesians 5, Galatians 5:22-23
