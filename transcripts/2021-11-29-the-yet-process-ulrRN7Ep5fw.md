# The 'Yet' Process

*2021-11-29 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulrRN7Ep5fw) — 00:46:40*

> **Our faith to say 'yet I will rejoice in the Lord' in present difficult circumstances is based on God's eternal character, His past faithfulness, and His future promises, even when we don't understand His current actions.**

## Summary

This sermon walks through the book of Habakkuk, showing how the prophet brings his complaints and frustrations about injustice directly to God, wrestles with God's unexpected answers, and through a process of questioning, waiting, and listening arrives at a faith that can say 'yet I will rejoice in the Lord' regardless of circumstances.

**Key points**

- Bring your pain and questions directly to God rather than comparing yourself to others
- God's judgment is executed on an eternal timeline, not our immediate expectations
- After bringing complaints to God, we must wait and listen for His response
- The righteous will live by faith, not by understanding everything
- Faith to say 'yet' in difficulty is grounded in God's character, past faithfulness, and future promises

**Scripture:** Habakkuk 1:1-4, Habakkuk 1:5-11, Habakkuk 1:12-2:1, Habakkuk 2:4, Habakkuk 2:14, Habakkuk 3:2, Habakkuk 3:17-19, Psalm 103:10
