# Hebrews with Dr. Moo

*2023-06-19 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMppRq-Kg0w) — 00:26:20*

> **Because Christ's sacrifice has granted us confident access to God's presence, we must respond by drawing near to God in faith, holding firm to hope in his promises, and spurring one another on in love through regular fellowship.**

## Summary

This sermon expounds Hebrews 10:19-25 as the central passage of the entire book, explaining how Christ's sacrifice has opened access to God's presence—symbolized by the torn curtain of the Old Testament tabernacle—and how believers should respond to that privilege. The structure moves from what God has done (verses 19-21) to three imperatives for Christian living: draw near to God, hold unswervingly to hope, and spur one another on in love.

**Key points**

- Christ as high priest has opened access to God's presence through the curtain (his body)
- Draw near to God with confidence based on what he has done (the backward look of faith)
- Hold unswervingly to the hope of God's promises for the future (the forward look)
- Spur one another on toward love and good deeds, meeting together regularly (the present focus)
- Faith, hope, and love form the triad of Christian virtues for our pilgrimage

**Scripture:** Hebrews 10:19-25, 1 John 4:19, Hebrews 9:19, Hebrews 6
