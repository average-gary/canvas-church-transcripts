# A Living Savior: PALM SUNDAY

*2024-03-25 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXUfdhA4XFg) — 00:41:55*

> **Jesus came not as the warrior-king Messiah Israel expected to defeat Rome, but as a suffering servant who conquered spiritual darkness through the cross and became the true temple where God dwells with humanity.**

## Summary

This Palm Sunday sermon examines the Jewish expectation of a warrior-king Messiah who would liberate Israel from Rome, contrasted with Jesus' actual mission to bring spiritual liberation through suffering and the cross. The preacher unpacks Holy Week chronologically and explores how the symbolism of palm branches (historically linked to military victory and temple cleansing) reveals the people's hopes for armed revolt, while Jesus instead fulfilled prophecy by entering Jerusalem as a humble king who would become the dwelling place of God himself rather than restore a physical temple.

**Key points**

- Overview of Holy Week from Palm Sunday through Resurrection Sunday
- First-century Jewish expectations for a warrior Messiah like David who would defeat Rome
- Palm branches symbolized military victory and temple cleansing in Jewish history (Maccabees)
- Jesus fulfilled Zechariah and Ezekiel's prophecies by entering from the east and cleansing the temple
- Jesus came to be the temple himself—dwelling with people, not in stone buildings
- The cross was God's upside-down victory over evil, not the military conquest people expected
