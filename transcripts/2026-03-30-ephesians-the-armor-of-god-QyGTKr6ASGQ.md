# EPHESIANS: The Armor of God

*2026-03-30 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QyGTKr6ASGQ) — 00:41:53*

> **The church is called to stand firm in the victory Christ already won through the cross, resisting evil not through violence or aggression but through faithfulness, peace, and prayerful dependence on God.**

## Summary

This Palm Sunday sermon on Ephesians 6:10-20 reframes the armor of God passage as a call to defensive faithfulness rather than spiritual aggression. The preacher emphasizes that the church stands in the victory Christ already won at the cross, resisting evil through peace, truth, and prayer rather than through violence or treating people as enemies.

**Key points**

- The dominant command is 'stand firm' in Christ's victory, not attack
- People are not our enemy; the struggle is spiritual, not against flesh and blood
- God himself is the warrior (Isaiah 59) who clothes his church in his own armor
- Truth and righteousness/justice together hold the church and guard what is central
- The sword of the Spirit is the Word of God—ultimately Jesus—not a weapon of violence

**Scripture:** Ephesians 6:10-20, Isaiah 59, Zechariah 9, John 1:1, Ephesians 2-3
