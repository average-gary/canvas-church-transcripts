# An Abiding Church

*2022-10-31 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yxfS0zkynMA) — 00:36:09*

> **Believers can only bear lasting fruit when they maintain dedicated dependence on Jesus through obedience to his command to love one another.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Jesus' metaphor of the vine and branches from John 15, calling believers to dedicated dependence on Christ. The speaker contrasts Jesus as the 'true vine' with Israel as the 'useless vine' apart from God, emphasizing that fruitfulness comes only through remaining connected to Jesus and obeying his command to love one another.

**Key points**

- Jesus is the true vine; we are branches that bear fruit only when connected to him
- Pruning by the Father makes us more fruitful by cutting away what hinders
- Remaining in Jesus means obeying his command to love one another
- The church exists not for self-preservation but to show God to the world
- Listening to Jesus requires a response—doing what he says, not just hearing it
