# NAMES OF GOD: Emmanuel

*2025-12-15 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDs16x40HWI) — 00:38:56*

> **True and everlasting joy comes from being in a safe, secure, face-to-face relationship with God, made possible through Jesus (Emmanuel, God with us) and sustained through the Holy Spirit's indwelling presence.**

## Summary

This sermon explores the biblical meaning of joy through the name Emmanuel (God with us), arguing that true joy is not merely happiness but the emotional response to being in a safe, secure, face-to-face relationship with someone who delights in you. Through neuroscience, attachment theory, and Scripture, the pastor demonstrates that everlasting joy is only found in God's presence—first through Jesus' incarnation and now through the Holy Spirit's indwelling.

**Key points**

- Joy is the experience of being with someone who is glad to be with you, requiring face-to-face relational presence
- Emmanuel (God with us) is the source of everlasting joy, coming near to restore our relationship with him
- Jesus brings joy through his incarnational presence, and the Holy Spirit through indwelling presence
- We often search for joy in wrong places: broken relationships, screens, substances—things that bring only temporary pleasure
- We must invest in face-to-face time with God and extend his joyful presence to others

**Scripture:** Luke 2:10, Matthew 1:23, Psalm 16:11, Psalm 21:6, John 15:10-11, 2 John 1:12, Hebrews 12:2
