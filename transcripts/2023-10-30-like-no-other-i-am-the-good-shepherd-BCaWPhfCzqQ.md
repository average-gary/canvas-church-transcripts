# Like No Other: I AM the Good Shepherd

*2023-10-30 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BCaWPhfCzqQ) — 00:41:05*

> **Jesus the good shepherd calls us to hear his voice and follow him into abundant life, which requires both taking responsibility for our interior world and remaining accountable within community.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Jesus's declaration 'I am the good shepherd' from John 10, unpacking three shepherd stories Jesus tells and what they reveal about his relationship with his followers. The preacher then shifts to a metaphor of a homestead representing our interior spiritual life, examining healthy and unhealthy relational boundaries and how we steward the life Christ offers.

**Key points**

- Jesus uses the culturally familiar shepherd imagery in three contexts: the communal town pen, the pasture pen where the shepherd is the door, and the open field with wolves
- Jesus makes the radical claim that the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep, blending what shepherds do with who God is
- We are called to hear and respond to the shepherd's voice, trusting he gives abundant life
- Our interior life is like a homestead that needs boundaries, stewardship, and selective access
- Healthy relationships balance personal responsibility for ourselves with accountability to others

**Scripture:** John 10:1-21, Psalm 23, Psalm 78:20, Psalm 79:13, Psalm 80:1, Psalm 100, Galatians 5:22-23
