# A SUMMER'S JOURNEY: Present Tense Salvation

*2024-08-12 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mF081V1nz0o) — 01:28:32*

> **Salvation is not merely a past decision but a present journey of sanctification where believers must daily die to their old selves and walk in faithful allegiance to Christ, being continually formed into His image through the testing and refining of the spiritual desert.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Romans 6 through the lens of Israel's Exodus journey, arguing that salvation has a present tense: believers are being saved through ongoing sanctification. Just as Israel passed through the Red Sea into the desert, Christians pass through baptism into a lifelong journey of dying to self and being formed into Christ's image.

**Key points**

- Baptism parallels Israel crossing the Red Sea—leaving bondage of the old life
- The desert journey represents present-tense sanctification, not just arrival at salvation
- Israel's testing and failures in the wilderness mirror our daily spiritual formation
- Sin no longer has dominion over believers, but we must work out salvation with fear and trembling
- Sanctification means embracing the desert where we learn to rely on Christ alone

**Scripture:** Romans 6:1-14, Exodus 14:19-23, Philippians 2:12-13, Genesis 1:28, 1 Corinthians 15, John 6, Galatians 2:20
