# FAQ: Christians & Politics

*2024-10-14 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nva5hH09D2w) — 01:02:06*

> **Christians must prioritize their citizenship in God's eternal kingdom over temporary political allegiances, actively seeking the flourishing of their communities through Christlike humility and service rather than worldly power.**

## Summary

This sermon addresses how Christians should engage with politics, arguing that believers are citizens of God's kingdom first and their earthly nation second. Christians are called to live as exiles who seek the flourishing of their communities through sacrificial service rather than worldly power, making them 'unreliable political partners' because kingdom values transcend partisan allegiances.

**Key points**

- Christians belong to God's kingdom first, before any earthly political allegiance
- Believers are exiles and foreigners called to seek the peace and prosperity of their cities
- The kingdom of God operates through humility and sacrifice, not worldly power
- Christians should be 'unreliable political partners' who follow kingdom values over party lines
- We must not grow weary in doing good for all people, including those we disagree with

**Scripture:** Philippians 2:1-11, 1 Peter 2:11, 1 Peter 4:7-11, Jeremiah 29:4-11, Galatians 6:8-10
