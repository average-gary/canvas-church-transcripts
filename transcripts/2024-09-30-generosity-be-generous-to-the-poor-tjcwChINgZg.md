# GENEROSITY: Be Generous to the Poor

*2024-09-30 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tjcwChINgZg) — 00:52:53*

> **Jesus calls us to reject the world's rules of hoarding wealth and instead embrace God's kingdom economy by living as generous stewards who prioritize meeting the needs of the poor.**

## Summary

This sermon argues that God designed creation with enough resources for everyone, but humanity has rebelled by hoarding wealth. Jesus invites followers into the kingdom of God by rejecting entitlement, excessive saving, and luxury spending, and instead giving generously to the poor as a way of aligning with God's original design and storing up eternal treasure.

**Key points**

- The world was created with abundant resources meant to be shared, but humanity redefined the rules to hoard for self
- Jesus announces the gospel of the kingdom, inviting people to play by God's rules instead of the world's
- Three symptoms of wrong rules: entitlement (believing we deserve what we have), saving more than we need, seeking temporary pleasure through possessions
- Regularly giving to the poor corrects these symptoms by reminding us everything is a gift and helping us ask 'should I?' instead of 'can I afford this?'
- Practical call to re-evaluate personal budgets according to kingdom principles, asking the Holy Spirit for direction

**Scripture:** Luke 12:13-21, Luke 12:33, Matthew 25, 1 John 3:16-18, Genesis 1-3, Ecclesiastes
