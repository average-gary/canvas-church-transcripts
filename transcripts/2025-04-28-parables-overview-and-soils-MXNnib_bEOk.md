# Parables: Overview and Soils

*2025-04-28 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MXNnib_bEOk) — 00:43:38*

> **True hearing of God's word requires open, obedient response that bears fruit through changed behavior and gospel witness, not merely intellectual understanding or emotional enthusiasm.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces an 8-week series on Jesus' parables by examining the Parable of the Sower from Mark 4. The preacher explains how parables work by setting up cultural expectations and then twisting them to prompt behavioral change, and demonstrates that this particular parable is about the necessity of truly hearing—meaning obeying—God's word so it can bear fruit in our lives.

**Key points**

- Parables use cultural tropes to set expectations, then twist them to get past hardened hearts and elicit response
- The phrase 'he who has ears to hear' signals the fulfillment of Isaiah's prophecy about a generation finally able to listen
- The four soils represent different responses: hard path (rejection), rocky ground (shallow joy without depth), thorny ground (growth choked by worldly cares), and good soil (obedient hearing that bears fruit)
- True hearing includes obeying and responding, not just intellectual understanding or emotional reaction
- Christian community prepares the soil of our hearts through confession, generosity, prayer, and shared life together

**Scripture:** Mark 4:1-23, Mark 3:9-21, Isaiah 6:9, Isaiah 27, Matthew 13:14-17, Matthew 7, John 15, 2 Samuel
