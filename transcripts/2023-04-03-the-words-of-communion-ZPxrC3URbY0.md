# The Words of Communion

*2023-04-03 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZPxrC3URbY0) — 01:02:14*

> **Communion is an act of active remembrance that calls us to contemplate God's faithfulness, examine ourselves honestly, repent without condemnation, and give thanks for Christ's sacrifice that unites us as one body.**

## Summary

This sermon explores the practice of communion through six interconnected words: contemplation, examination, repentance, forgiveness, unity, and eucharist (thanksgiving). The speaker emphasizes that biblical 'remembrance' is not merely cognitive acknowledgment but an active recalling of God's faithfulness and Christ's sacrifice, using examples like Joshua's rock piles in the Jordan River that become visible during drought as reminders in difficult times. The sermon calls believers to strip away the domestication of communion and grasp the full weight of what Jesus endured.

**Key points**

- Biblical remembrance is active and transformative, not just mental acknowledgment
- Communion involves contemplation, examination, repentance, forgiveness, unity, and thanksgiving
- God sets up memorials (like rock piles and the rainbow) so we remember His faithfulness in hard times
- Self-examination should be done without condemnation, setting our minds on what the Spirit desires
- We must understand the true cost of Christ's sacrifice to fully appreciate the resurrection

**Scripture:** Genesis 6:17, Exodus 20:8, Romans 8, 1 Corinthians 11
