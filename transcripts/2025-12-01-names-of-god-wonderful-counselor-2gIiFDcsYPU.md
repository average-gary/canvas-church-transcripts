# NAMES OF GOD: Wonderful Counselor

*2025-12-01 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2gIiFDcsYPU) — 00:23:37*

> **Jesus as our Wonderful Counselor both declares the difficult path that leads to true joy and resurrection life, and goes before us to show us the way, giving us confident hope that following him leads to victory.**

## Summary

This Advent sermon explores Jesus as 'Wonderful Counselor,' the first title from Isaiah's prophecy, using the Bluey episode 'The Creek' to illustrate how Jesus invites us on a difficult but beautiful path toward true joy. The sermon emphasizes that Jesus not only gives us direction through his teaching but also leads by example, particularly in loving enemies and enduring suffering.

**Key points**

- Jesus as Wonderful Counselor gives direction that leads to joy, not just worldly wisdom
- Jesus tells us the way through teachings like the Sermon on the Mount—turn the other cheek, love your enemies
- Jesus leads the way by example, demonstrating perfect obedience even to death on the cross
- The path of discipleship is difficult but beautiful, proven victorious through the resurrection
- This first week of Advent focuses on hope as confident expectation that Jesus will fulfill his promises

**Scripture:** Isaiah 9:6-7, Matthew 5-7, Hebrews 12:1-2
