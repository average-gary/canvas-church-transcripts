# PTW Sabbath: STOP

*2026-01-19 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n5ShVUQo71s) — 00:41:06*

> **Sabbath is God's invitation woven into creation for us to stop producing and find our identity not in what we do but in being His image-bearers, offering both personal soul-rest and counter-cultural witness in an anxious, overworked society.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces Sabbath as a spiritual practice woven into creation from Genesis, designed to counter our culture's busyness and productivity obsession. The pastor argues that Sabbath is not an outdated Old Testament rule but an ongoing invitation from God to stop working, rest, delight, and worship, reminding us that our value comes from being God's image-bearers rather than what we produce or consume.

**Key points**

- Sabbath is a rhythm built into the fabric of creation itself
- It's a counter-formational practice against cultural pressures of productivity and FOMO
- The Sabbath command calls us to remember our identity as image-bearers, not slaves
- Practical invitation to work toward a weekly 24-hour period of rest, delight, and worship
- Sabbath is a foretaste of eternal rest in new creation

**Scripture:** Genesis 1:14, Genesis 2:1-3, Exodus 20:8-11, Leviticus 23, Matthew 11:28-30, Revelation 21:22-23
