# REVELATION: Escaping Babylon

*2026-08-24 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ngzo9otJzuQ) — 01:04:32*

> **Believers must disentangle themselves from the seductive worldly systems that exploit people and compete with allegiance to God by placing their hope in Christ alone rather than in wealth, pleasure, or cultural acceptance.**

## Summary

This sermon on Revelation 17-18 examines Babylon as a symbol of corrupt worldly systems—economic, political, and cultural—that seduce people through materialism, pleasure, and exploitation of human lives. The preacher calls believers to recognize their entanglement with these systems and to 'come out' by placing their hope and love in Christ alone rather than in wealth, cultural acceptance, or comfort.

**Key points**

- Babylon represents seductive, idolatrous culture characterized by exploitation and the destruction of human lives
- The beast symbolizes military and political power that works in tandem with corrupt cultural and religious systems
- Two responses to Babylon's fall: mourning its loss (being entangled) or rejoicing in God's justice (being separate)
- Modern believers become entangled through pursuing financial security, cultural acceptance, pleasure, and self-focused living
- Disentanglement requires placing hope in Christ alone and living with generosity, justice, humility, and self-control

**Scripture:** Revelation 17:1-6, Revelation 18:1-8, Revelation 13, Revelation 16:19, Philippians 3:7-8, Philippians 3:18, James 1:27, 1 Peter 2:11, Hebrews 12:1-3
