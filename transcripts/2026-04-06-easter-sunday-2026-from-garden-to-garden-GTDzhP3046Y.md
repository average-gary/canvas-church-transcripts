# Easter Sunday 2026: From Garden to Garden

*2026-04-06 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GTDzhP3046Y) — 00:51:03*

> **Jesus's resurrection launches new creation and restores the Garden of Eden, inviting humanity to participate in God's renewal of all creation here and now, not just securing a future destination after death.**

## Summary

This Easter sermon argues that the resurrection is not merely about personal salvation or going to heaven, but about Jesus launching new creation and restoring the Garden of Eden. Using John's gospel, the preacher traces deliberate parallels between Genesis and the resurrection account to show that Jesus is the new Adam who succeeded where the first Adam failed, and that resurrection life is breaking into the present world through the power of the Holy Spirit.

**Key points**

- John's gospel deliberately echoes the Genesis creation account to frame Jesus's resurrection as new creation dawning
- Jesus is the new Adam who takes on the curse (thorns, death) in the desert and overcomes it
- The resurrection happened in a garden on the first day of the week, signaling the restart of creation
- Jesus breathed on the disciples just as God breathed life into Adam, giving them the Holy Spirit
- Jesus meets Mary in her grief, Thomas in his doubt, and Peter in his shame, calling each by name and offering restoration

**Scripture:** Genesis 1:1-3, Genesis 1:27, Genesis 2, Genesis 3, John 1:1-5, John 19:5, John 19, John 20, John 21:15
