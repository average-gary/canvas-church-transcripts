# Spirit Led Life

*2021-10-11 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MiM8JvFExPE) — 00:49:18*

> **Pride destroys us by making us believe we are in control, but when we repent and acknowledge God's sovereignty with the Holy Spirit's help, He delivers and restores us.**

## Summary

This sermon examines Daniel 4, where King Nebuchadnezzar receives a warning dream about his pride through Daniel's interpretation. Despite Daniel's tender rebuke urging repentance, Nebuchadnezzar boasts of his accomplishments and is struck with insanity for seven years until he acknowledges God's sovereignty and is restored.

**Key points**

- Daniel's consistent walk with God gave him Holy Spirit power that surpassed all human wisdom
- Daniel delivered hard correction with a tender, broken heart—loving his enemy despite thirty years of captivity
- Nebuchadnezzar's pride led him to credit himself rather than God, resulting in seven years of madness
- When God is not our primary focus, we fall into destructive, animalistic patterns
- Repentance and humility restore us—deliverance comes when we turn our eyes back to Jesus
