# FAQ: What's up with New Heavens & New Earth?

*2024-11-18 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2DUboSiTcfw) — 01:00:23*

> **God's ultimate plan is not to evacuate humanity to a disembodied heaven but to unite heaven and earth in a renewed creation where he dwells with his resurrected people in glorified bodies.**

## Summary

This sermon challenges the popular notion that Christians simply 'go to heaven' as disembodied souls when they die. Instead, it argues from Scripture that God's plan has always been to unite heaven and earth in a renewed creation where he dwells with resurrected, embodied humanity. The fall separated these realms, but through Jesus and the church, heaven is already invading earth in anticipation of the final resurrection and cosmic renewal.

**Key points**

- Popular views of heaven as escape to the clouds contradict the biblical narrative
- God created heaven and earth to be united; the fall separated them
- God maintained his presence on earth through tabernacle, temple, and ultimately Jesus
- Believers are now temples where heaven meets earth in the 'already not yet' kingdom
- Revelation shows new Jerusalem coming down to earth, not souls going up to heaven
