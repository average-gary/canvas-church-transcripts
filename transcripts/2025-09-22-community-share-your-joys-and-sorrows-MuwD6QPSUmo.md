# COMMUNITY: Share Your Joys and Sorrows

*2025-09-22 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MuwD6QPSUmo) — 00:47:58*

> **Followers of Jesus must intentionally build authentic Christian community around the table where they share both their joys and sorrows, following Christ's example of bringing the full range of his emotions to his disciples.**

## Summary

This sermon calls believers to intentional Christian community centered around the table, where they share both joys and sorrows authentically. Using Jesus' example at the Last Supper and in Gethsemane, the pastor argues that spiritual formation requires moving beyond shallow conversations to vulnerable relationships where people celebrate God's goodness together and bear one another's burdens in suffering.

**Key points**

- Modern loneliness epidemic requires intentional, embodied community around the table
- Jesus modeled defiant joy at the Last Supper despite impending betrayal and death
- Jesus invited his closest disciples into his suffering and sorrow at Gethsemane
- We must move past shallow talk and spiritual platitudes to real vulnerability
- Application: Share highs and lows (joys and sorrows) with Christian community weekly

**Scripture:** Mark 14:18-26, Mark 14:32-36, John 15, Romans 12:9-21, Galatians 5:22, Philippians 4:4-8, Hebrews 3:12-13, James 1:17-18
