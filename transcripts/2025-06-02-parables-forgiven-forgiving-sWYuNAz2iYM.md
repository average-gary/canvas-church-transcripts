# PARABLES: Forgiven & Forgiving

*2025-06-02 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sWYuNAz2iYM) — 00:55:32*

> **Because followers of Jesus have been forgiven an unpayable debt, they must extend that same mercy to others—not because people deserve it, but because we didn't deserve it either.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Jesus' parable of the unforgiving servant in Matthew 18 to teach that forgiveness is not optional but essential in God's kingdom. Corey Smith contrasts two kingdoms—the world's system of scorekeeping and retribution versus heaven's economy of grace and mercy—arguing that those who have received God's undeserved forgiveness must extend that same mercy to others.

**Key points**

- In the world's kingdom hurt people hurt people, but in God's kingdom forgiven people forgive people
- Jesus redefines greatness as humility and servanthood, not dominance or power
- The parable contrasts an unpayable debt forgiven by a king with a servant's refusal to forgive a small debt
- Grace creates relational obligation—recipients are called to extend what they have received
- Forgiveness is not optional or formulaic but a lifestyle that reveals true salvation

**Scripture:** Matthew 18:21-35, 2 Corinthians 5:20
