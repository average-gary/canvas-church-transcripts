# The Almighty Dollar: KINGDOM VALUES

*2026-04-20 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YHCaW5DAEj4) — 00:43:46*

> **Following Christ requires repenting from trust in wealth and adopting God's values by caring for those society overlooks, storing up treasure in heaven rather than on earth.**

## Summary

This sermon examines the story of the rich young ruler from Mark 10 to challenge the congregation's relationship with money and values. The preacher argues that Christ isn't condemning wealth itself but the idolatry of trusting in it and letting it define what we value, calling believers instead to value what God values—particularly the vulnerable and overlooked in society.

**Key points**

- Christ frequently discusses money because we are preoccupied with it, using our language to address spiritual matters
- The rich young ruler's problem was serving his wealth and letting it define his values, not simply having wealth
- Jesus challenges us to value those society overlooks—the poor, vulnerable, and marginalized
- The disciples were shocked to learn wealth doesn't equal righteousness or guarantee entry into God's kingdom
- True treasure is found in heaven through following Christ and participating in His kingdom community
- The early church embodied this through koinonia, sharing all things in common, exemplified by Barnabas

**Scripture:** Mark 10:17-31, Leviticus 19:18, Deuteronomy 6:5, Matthew 6:19-21, Matthew 6:24, Matthew 6:28, Matthew 18-20, Acts 4:32-37, 1 Corinthians 10:14-17, 1 Corinthians 11:23-26
