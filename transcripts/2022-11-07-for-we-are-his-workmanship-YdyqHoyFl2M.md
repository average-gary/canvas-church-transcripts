# For We Are HIS Workmanship

*2022-11-07 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YdyqHoyFl2M) — 00:43:12*

> **We are God's collective masterpiece, created by grace not just for individual salvation but to display His redemptive glory as a kingdom outpost welcoming others into His work.**

## Summary

This sermon unpacks Ephesians 2:10 and the meaning of 'poiema' (workmanship/canvas), explaining why the church chose 'Canvas' as their name. The pastor emphasizes that believers are God's collective masterpiece—His second creation after the physical world—designed to display His redemptive glory and serve as a kingdom outpost with radical hospitality.

**Key points**

- The word 'poiema' (workmanship/canvas) appears twice in the NT: God's creation in Romans 1:20 and the new creation (church) in Ephesians 2:10
- Ephesians 2:10 links individual salvation by grace through faith (verses 8-9) with collective mission and identity
- God creates two canvases: the first shows His power as Creator, the second shows His love as Redeemer through the church
- Salvation is communal ('we are') not merely individual—believers form a kingdom outpost in a fallen world
- The church is called to radical hospitality, welcoming people to belong before they believe, following St. Patrick's missionary model

**Scripture:** Ephesians 2:8-10, Romans 1:20, Ephesians 2:19-22
