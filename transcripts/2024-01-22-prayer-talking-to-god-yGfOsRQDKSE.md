# PRAYER: Talking to God

*2024-01-22 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yGfOsRQDKSE) — 01:23:14*

> **Prayer becomes a delight rather than a duty when we daily remind ourselves that God is our loving, present, holy Father and that we are His children who depend on His provision, forgiveness, and victory.**

## Summary

This sermon explores the Lord's Prayer as a model for transforming prayer from duty into delight by reminding ourselves daily of God's character and our dependence on Him. The pastor unpacks how Jesus teaches us to approach God as a loving, present, holy Father while acknowledging our need for His provision, forgiveness, and victory over temptation.

**Key points**

- God is our loving Father (Abba), not a distant deity requiring performance
- God is closer than we think—omnipresent and always available
- God is holy and unchanging in His perfect character
- We need God's daily provision, forgiveness, and power to overcome temptation
- Prayer partners us with God's plan to restore His will on earth as in heaven

**Scripture:** Matthew 6, Luke 15, Exodus 34:6, Matthew 6:25-33, Isaiah 6
