# Fruit Bulb Moment

*2022-06-06 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kcn8tEtIf4k) — 00:48:46*

> **Christians cannot manufacture spiritual fruit through their own effort, but they can partner with the Holy Spirit by creating a hospitable environment in their lives for God to produce the character of Jesus.**

## Summary

This sermon introduces a series on the fruit of the Spirit from Galatians 5, addressing the tension every Christian faces between the Spirit's desires and sinful nature. Using the metaphor of gardening, the pastor explains that believers cannot produce spiritual fruit themselves but can create a hospitable environment for the Holy Spirit to cultivate Christ-like character in their lives.

**Key points**

- Every Christian struggles with tension between Spirit and flesh
- Legalism and license both fail to resolve this tension
- God plants and grows the fruit; we tend the environment
- Remove sinful attitudes and add spiritual disciplines
- Becoming like Jesus is a lifelong process

**Scripture:** Galatians 5:16-26, Romans 7, Genesis 3, 1 John 3:16
