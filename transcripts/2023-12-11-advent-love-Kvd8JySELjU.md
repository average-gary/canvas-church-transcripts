# ADVENT: Love

*2023-12-11 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kvd8JySELjU) — 00:12:32*

> **Because God first loved us by sending Jesus, we are called to complete that love by serving and loving others in response.**

## Summary

This Advent sermon on love emphasizes that God demonstrated his love by sending Jesus into the world, and that experiencing God's love compels believers to love and serve others in return. The preacher argues that God's love is made complete in us when we share it through acts of service, making love more than just a feeling but a cycle of receiving and giving.

**Key points**

- God's greatest love story is sending Jesus to save the world
- We must both experience God's love and express it through service to others
- God's love in us is incomplete until we demonstrate it by loving others
- The more we understand God's love for us, the more we're compelled to share it
- Serving others should flow from recognizing how blessed we are by God's love

**Scripture:** John 3:16, 1 John 4:9-12, 1 John 4:19
