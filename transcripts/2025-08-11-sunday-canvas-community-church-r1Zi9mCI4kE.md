## Summary

Jill Keister shares her family's stressful house-selling experience as a framework for exploring Psalm 143, a psalm of lament. She demonstrates how David's knowledge of God's character enabled him to pray boldly even in deep distress, and challenges the congregation to meditate on God's past faithfulness so they can trust Him in present trials and become more aware of His presence in daily life.

**Key points**

- Psalm 143 is a lament where David cries out boldly because he knows God's faithful character
- Remembering and meditating on God's past works shapes our prayer life and gives us hope
- Physical posture affects spiritual position - extending our hands to God in trust and surrender
- We must become more aware of God's presence working all around us
- Knowing God's character changes how we live and pray
