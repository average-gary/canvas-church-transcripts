# Spiritual Flourishing

*2022-02-28 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL1q8BRw614) — 00:46:48*

> **God invites believers not to mere moral behavior modification but to spiritual flourishing that transforms from the inside out through receiving and applying His word of truth.**

## Summary

This sermon from James 1:16-25 contrasts two versions of Christianity that look similar externally but differ in substance: external moral behavior modification versus internal spiritual transformation. The pastor warns against being deceived into mere morality when believers have been invited into spiritual flourishing that starts from the heart and works outward.

**Key points**

- Two types of following Jesus look similar on the surface but differ in quality and foundation
- Transformation begins by receiving the 'word of truth' as an implanted seed, not by behavior modification
- God's word functions as a mirror revealing heart condition, not just correcting external behavior
- The application pattern: assess what God's word reveals, agree with His way, apply through repentance, repeat
- Building life on Jesus' foundation withstands trials, while building on external morality collapses under pressure

**Scripture:** James 1:16-25, Jeremiah 31:33, Matthew 7:24-27, Matthew 5-7
