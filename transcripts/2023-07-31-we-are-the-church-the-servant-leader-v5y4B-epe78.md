# We Are the Church: The Servant Leader

*2023-07-31 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v5y4B-epe78) — 00:50:05*

> **Christ calls us to servant leadership that balances authority, responsibility, and privilege so we can reproduce lasting fruit by making others into Christ-like leaders.**

## Summary

This sermon explores the biblical model of servant leadership, arguing that effective Christian leaders must balance three elements: responsibility, authority, and privilege. Using Philippians 2 and John 15 as anchor texts, the speaker contends that Jesus exemplified this balance by emptying himself as a servant yet leading with divine authority, and now calls his followers to the same pattern. The message warns against various dysfunctional leadership styles that emerge when these three elements fall out of alignment, emphasizing that the ultimate goal of servant leadership is not merely producing results but reproducing Christ-like leaders in others.

**Key points**

- Servant leadership is not either/or but both/and: we serve while we lead
- Authority, responsibility, and privilege must stay in balance for healthy leadership
- Imbalances create dysfunction: narcissism, codependency, micromanagement, burnout, or hireling mentality
- The highest level of leadership is reproduction—making others better leaders, not just getting results
- Self-care is stewardship, not selfishness; leaders cannot give what they have not received

**Scripture:** Philippians 2:5-11, John 15:9-17, 2 Timothy 2, Romans 8:28-29
