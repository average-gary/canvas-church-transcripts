> **Jesus is the Good Shepherd who intimately knows His sheep, calls them by name, and leads them through both blessing and hardship toward the abundant life He promises.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Jesus as the Good Shepherd from John 10, showing how He knows His sheep by name and they recognize His voice. It connects this teaching to Psalm 23, emphasizing that God leads through both green pastures and dark valleys, ultimately promising an abundant life that culminates in the heavenly vision of Revelation 7.

**Key points**

- Jesus as the Good Shepherd who calls His sheep by name
- Believers recognize and follow the shepherd's voice
- Psalm 23's path includes both still waters and the valley of the shadow of death
- In the darkest valleys, God is closest—close enough to talk to
- God's goodness and mercy actively pursue us all our days
- Abundant life means provision, peace, and assurance of a heavenly home
