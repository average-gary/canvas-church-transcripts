# Successfully Humility

*2022-05-23 — [watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HO-bPbRzHeQ) — 00:45:57*

> **Successful Christian life and church are assembled with an attitude of humility that mirrors Christ, who gave up his divine privileges, reputation, wealth, and strength to sacrificially serve others.**

## Summary

This sermon explores Philippians 2:3-11, arguing that successful Christian life and church are built on humility. The pastor walks through Paul's instructions on selfless service, Jesus' example of giving up divine privileges from incarnation to crucifixion, and the ultimate exaltation that results from humility.

**Key points**

- Don't serve your own interests; selflessly serve others all the time
- Three obstacles to humility: wealth, reputation/status, and strength
- Jesus demonstrated perfect humility from his birth through his death on the cross
- Christ gave up reputation, wealth, and divine privileges to serve humanity
- True joy comes from Christ's exaltation, not from climbing society's ladder

**Scripture:** Philippians 2:3-4, Philippians 2:5-11, Mark 10:21-23, Mark 10:35-38, Mark 14:46-47, John 3:30, John 13
