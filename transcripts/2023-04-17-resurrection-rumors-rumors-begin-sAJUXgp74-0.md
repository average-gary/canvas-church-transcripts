> **In the midst of debilitating worry and fear, acknowledge God's sovereign lordship, let love motivate faithfulness, and practice relinquishment by surrendering your circumstances into His good and capable hands.**

## Summary

This sermon addresses how to respond to debilitating worry, anxiety, and fear by examining Mary Magdalene's encounter with the risen Jesus. The pastor teaches that God often allows visions and dreams to die before resurrecting them so He receives the glory, and concludes with a practical exercise in the prayer of relinquishment.

**Key points**

- Acknowledge God's lordship does not depend on your understanding of circumstances
- Let love motivate you even when confused and fearful
- Have an ear to hear the Lord's voice when He speaks
- Obey what God has already told you rather than seeking new revelation
- Practice the four-step prayer of relinquishment to surrender fear and anxiety
